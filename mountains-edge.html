Mountain's Edge trades the golf course for parks

Mountain's Edge is a large master plan in the southwest valley, mostly in 89178 with a piece in 89179, built from roughly 2005 through 2018 along Blue Diamond Road and Buffalo Drive. It was planned around open space rather than a private club, and that decision shapes everything about the community.

Exploration Peak Park and the Mountain's Edge Regional Park sit inside the community, with trails, sports fields, and an actual hill with a view at the top. The trail system stitches the villages together, so you can walk between neighborhoods rather than driving the arterial. There is no guard gate and no private golf. What you get instead is more house for the money.

There is real choice here, and it still moves

141 single-family homes for sale is a real selection — more than the 134 in Southern Highlands on the same day — and 93 of them are priced between $400,000 and $600,000. Choice does not mean homes sit, though. The 186 that sold between May 14 and November 10, 2026 spent a median of 25 days on the market. What it means practically is that you can compare several homes side by side, but when the right one comes up you should be set up to see it quickly and be in a position to move. That is a strategy conversation, not a search problem, and it is worth having before you start looking.

Drive times, honestly

About 20 to 25 minutes to the Strip and roughly 20 minutes to Harry Reid International Airport outside of rush hour, mostly on the 215. Ten to fifteen minutes to the shopping and restaurants along Rainbow and the 215. It is well placed for the west and southwest side of town and poorly placed for Henderson, which is a solid 30 minutes plus.

HOAs — what to expect

Mountain's Edge homes carry a master association fee that funds the parks and trail system, and individual villages may add their own. The fees here are generally modest compared with the gated communities nearby, which is part of the value story. I pull the exact fee and the resale package on anything you are serious about so the number is not a surprise at closing.

Schools

The community is served by the Clark County School District, with elementary schools inside the master plan. Zoning is by street address and does get redrawn, so if schools matter to your decision Confirm the zoned schools for any address with the Clark County School District before you write an offer, and I'll help you look it up.

Who Mountain's Edge is right for

It fits buyers who want square footage, a yard, and parks they can walk to, and who would rather put the money into the house than into a gate and a golf membership. It fits buyers who work on the west or southwest side. It is the wrong fit if you want a guard gate, a golf course view, or a short hop to Henderson.

Questions I get about Mountain's Edge

How much does a home in Mountain's Edge cost?

On November 10, 2026 there were 141 single-family homes for sale in Mountain's Edge, from $335,000 to $1,479,999, with a median asking price of $499,999. 93 of them fell between $400,000 and $600,000. Single-family homes that sold between May 14 and November 10, 2026 closed at a median of $476,500.

Is Mountain's Edge a good value?

On asking price, it is the lowest-priced of the nine neighborhood guides I updated on November 10, 2026. On November 10, 2026 the median single-family home for sale here was asking $499,999 for a median 2,255 square feet, against a median of about $942,500 for 3,058 square feet in gated, golf-centered Southern Highlands. You are trading a guard gate and a golf course for price and parks.

What is there to do in Mountain's Edge?

It is built around open space rather than a golf course. Exploration Peak Park and the Mountain's Edge Regional Park sit inside the master plan, with trails, playing fields and a real hill to climb, and the trail system links neighborhoods together. That park network is the reason many buyers choose it.

How far is Mountain's Edge from the Strip and the airport?

Roughly 20 to 25 minutes to the Strip and about 20 minutes to Harry Reid International Airport outside of rush hour, via the 215. It is a southwest-valley location, so it is convenient to the west side and less convenient to Henderson.
