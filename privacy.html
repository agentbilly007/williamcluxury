Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how William Crawford ("we," "I") handles information collected through williamcluxury.com (the "Site").

Information we collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through contact and home-valuation forms — such as your name, email, phone number, and any details you share about your property or home search. We also collect standard analytics data (such as pages visited and general location) through Google Analytics to understand how the Site is used.

How we use it

Information you submit is used solely to respond to your inquiry, provide real estate services, and follow up about buying or selling a home. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-party services

The Site uses Google Analytics and a form-handling service (Web3Forms) to deliver form submissions. These providers process data according to their own privacy policies. Links to external sites (such as our brokerage's home search) are governed by those sites' policies.

Your choices

You may request that we delete the contact information you've submitted at any time by emailing billy@huntingtonandellis.com. You can opt out of analytics tracking using standard browser controls or the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Call (725) 567-9482 or email billy@huntingtonandellis.com.
