Buying a home with crypto is mostly about timing and paperwork. Here are the two ways it works, what changes when you have a loan, and the steps that keep your money safe.

Two ways to pay with crypto

  • Sell it for dollars first (the common way): you sell the crypto on your exchange, move the dollars to your bank, and wire them to the title or escrow company before closing. To the seller and escrow, this works like any other cash or loan purchase.
  • Pay the seller in crypto (rarer): the seller has to agree to take it, and you need a title or escrow company willing to handle it, or a crypto payment processor that converts it to dollars at closing. The buyer and the seller should each get their own legal and tax advice before agreeing to this.

If you're getting a loan

Lenders need to see where your down payment came from and that it has been in your account. Fannie Mae's Selling Guide allows crypto that has been exchanged into U.S. dollars for the down payment, closing costs and reserves, as long as there is documented evidence of the exchange, the dollars are held in a U.S. or state regulated financial institution, and the funds are verified in dollars before the loan closes.

Fannie Mae also says a large deposit may come from crypto that was sold, and the lender must get enough documents to show the money came from your crypto account. Crypto cannot be used for the earnest money deposit on the purchase.

That usually means statements from your exchange showing the crypto you held, the sale, and the transfer to your bank. FHA, VA and other loans have their own rules. Ask your lender what documents they need before you sell anything. I can share names of local lenders, and you choose.

Taxes

The IRS treats digital assets as property, not currency. When you sell crypto or exchange it for other property, you can have a capital gain or loss for federal tax, and sales get reported on Form 8949. Nevada's constitution bars a state tax on personal income, so there is no Nevada state income tax.

What you owe depends on your own situation. Check with your tax professional or CPA before you sell.

Price swings before closing

Crypto prices can move between the day you make an offer and the day you close. Many buyers convert to dollars before they write the offer, so the amount they have to spend is fixed. If you plan to sell later in the process, talk it through with your lender and CPA first.

Never trust wiring instructions that arrive by email alone. Before you send money, call the title company at a phone number you look up yourself, not one from an email, and confirm the instructions. The FBI and the CFPB both give this advice.

Steps, in order

  • Talk to your CPA about the tax side of selling.
  • If you're using a loan, ask your lender what documents they need, then get pre-approved.
  • Decide when to convert, and keep every exchange and bank statement.
  • Tour homes and write the offer. Here's how buying from out of state works if you're not local yet.
  • Confirm wiring instructions by phone with the title company, then wire your funds.

This is general information, not legal, tax or financial advice. Talk to your CPA, lender and a Nevada real estate attorney.

Questions people ask

Can I buy a house in Las Vegas with Bitcoin?

Yes, in two ways. Most often, you sell the crypto, move the dollars to your bank, and wire them to the title or escrow company before closing. Less often, a seller agrees to take crypto directly, which needs a title or escrow company or a crypto payment processor willing to handle it.

Can I use crypto for a down payment with a mortgage?

Fannie Mae's Selling Guide allows crypto that has been exchanged into U.S. dollars for the down payment, closing costs and reserves, if the dollars are held in a U.S. or state regulated financial institution and verified before closing. It cannot be used for the earnest money deposit. Ask your lender what documents they need.

Do I pay taxes when I sell crypto to buy a house?

The IRS treats digital assets as property, so selling or spending them can create a capital gain or loss that is reported on Form 8949. Nevada has no state income tax. Talk to your CPA about your own situation.

What if the price of Bitcoin changes before closing?

Crypto prices can move between your offer and closing. Many buyers convert to dollars before they write the offer, so the amount they have to spend is fixed.

How do I wire money safely to escrow?

Call the title company at a phone number you look up yourself, not one from an email, and confirm the wiring instructions before you send anything.
