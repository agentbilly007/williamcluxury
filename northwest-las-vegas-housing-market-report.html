How months of inventory is figured

Months of inventory = homes for sale today ÷ homes sold in the last 30 days.

For Northwest Las Vegas: 1,123 ÷ 199 = 5.6 months. It is how long the homes for sale today would last if sales kept the same pace and no new homes were listed. It describes the last 30 days only and does not say what prices will do next.
