Guard-gated communities in Henderson include MacDonald Highlands, Ascaya, Anthem Country Club and Tuscany, and there are guard-gated or gated sections inside Seven Hills, Green Valley South, Madeira Canyon, SouthShore at Lake Las Vegas, Sun City Anthem and Solera. On November 16, 2026, 277 single-family homes in guard-gated communities were for sale in Henderson. William Crawford, a Las Vegas REALTOR®, tours these homes on video for buyers who can't be here.

Guard-gated at the entrance

  • MacDonald Highlands. Custom homes in the foothills, with DragonRidge Country Club. Yes. It has two 24-hour guard-gated entries.
  • Ascaya. Custom estate homes on 664 acres in the McCullough Range. Yes. Ascaya describes itself as a private, guard-gated community.
  • Anthem Country Club. The private golf enclave at the top of Anthem. Yes. The HOA says the front gate is staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
  • Tuscany. East Henderson, built around Chimera Golf Club and a recreation center. Yes. The homeowners association describes a guarded gate entrance.

Communities with a gated section

  • Seven Hills. Several villages are guard-gated, such as Renaissance; others have open streets. See the village list on my Seven Hills guide.
  • Green Valley South. Parts of it. The City of Henderson marks the Copper Ridge and Tapatio neighborhoods inside it as gated.
  • Madeira Canyon. Part of it. The Club at Madeira Canyon is guard-gated.
  • SouthShore at Lake Las Vegas. Part of it. La Cova has a guard-protected entrance.
  • Sun City Anthem (55+). Pinnacle Village, 227 of the homes, is a gated neighborhood inside Sun City Anthem, according to the community association.
  • Solera at Anthem (55+). The Villas at Solera section is a gated neighborhood, according to the Las Vegas Review-Journal.

Gate staffing, hours and guest rules are set by each HOA and can change. Ask the HOA, or ask me to check, for any home you're serious about.

What a guard gate changes for you

  • HOA fees. Gate staff and private amenities usually mean a higher HOA fee. I pull the current fees and the resale package for any home you're serious about. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).
  • Visitors and deliveries. Each community has its own guest list and delivery rules.
  • Showings. I arrange gate access for our tours.
