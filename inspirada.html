About Inspirada

Inspirada is a master-planned community in Henderson. Its parks are the center of the plan: seven of them, named Attesa, Aventura, Capriola, Montagna, Potenza, Solista and Sentiero. Sentiero was built as the seventh and final park, and it and Attesa each have a pool for residents.

The community sits at the edge of Sloan Canyon National Conservation Area, known for its hiking and its petroglyphs.

HOAs

HOA fees can be different from one street to the next, so I don't quote one number. I pull the exact fees for any home you're serious about and go through the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Schools

Inspirada is in the Clark County School District, which assigns schools by street address. Check the zoning for any address with the Clark County School District before you write an offer. I'll help you look it up.
