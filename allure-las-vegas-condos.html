What Allure Las Vegas is

Allure is a single 41-story tower that was completed in 2007, with residents moving in at the end of that year. A second tower was planned but never built. Sources list about 428 units. These are full-time condos, not hotel units.

What's for sale today

On November 21, 2026, 23 units were for sale at Allure Las Vegas (1 studio, 5 one-bedroom, 11 two-bedroom, 6 three-bedroom), from $214,900 to $1,400,000. The median asking price was $399,900. Listed floors ran from 2 to 40.

HOA fees

On the 23 active listings, the total monthly HOA (all associations combined) ran from $497 to $1,886, with a median of $1,149. Studios: median $497. One-bedrooms: median $818. Two-bedrooms: median $1,149. Three-bedrooms: median $1,371. Listings most often show the fee covering building management, concierge, grounds upkeep and gated entry. Views and amenities

Views depend on the floor and which way the unit faces. On the 23 active listings with views entered, listing agents marked views of the city (22), the Strip (19), the mountains (17) and the pool (3). Many units list more than one. I check the direction and the floor for every unit we look at.

Building features most often listed in the MLS for Allure Las Vegas: business center, pool, guard-gated entry, fitness center, concierge, barbecue area, pet park and security. Confirm what is open to owners for the unit you are buying.

Rental rules

Rental rules at Allure Las Vegas are set by the HOA, including any limits on short-term rentals and minimum lease terms. Local short-term rental rules also apply. Read the CC&Rs and the current rules before you buy if you might rent the unit out.

On November 21, 2026, 4 of the 23 active listings were offered with furniture included.

Financing

Lenders review the building as well as the buyer for a condo loan, for example the HOA budget and reserves. If you plan to use a loan, ask your lender to look at this building early. I can share names of local lenders, and you choose.

Location

The tower sits at 200 W Sahara Ave, 89102, just west of Las Vegas Blvd at the north end of the Strip. I-15 is a few blocks west, and downtown is a short drive north.
