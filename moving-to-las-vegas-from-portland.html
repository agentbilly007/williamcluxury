Oregon and Nevada are almost opposites on taxes: Oregon has an income tax and no sales tax, and Nevada has a sales tax and no income tax. Which one costs you less depends on your income and how you spend. Here's how they compare.

Weather

Based on NOAA's 1991 to 2020 climate normals, Portland International Airport averages 36.92 inches of rain a year, and Las Vegas averages 4.18 inches. You trade gray winters for hot summers, so plan for higher air conditioning bills in July and August.

Taxes

  • Income: Oregon's top rate is 9.9% for 2026, on income over $125,000 for single filers and $250,000 for joint filers (Oregon Department of Revenue). Portland-area residents can also owe the Metro and Multnomah County income taxes (City of Portland). Nevada's constitution bars a state tax on the wages or personal income of individuals.
  • Capital gains: Oregon taxes most capital gains at the same rates as other income (Oregon Department of Revenue). Nevada has no state capital gains tax.
  • Estate tax: Oregon's estate tax applies to estates of $1 million or more (Oregon Department of Revenue). Nevada has none.
  • Sales tax: Oregon has no general sales tax. Clark County, Nevada's sales tax is 8.375%.

Everyone's tax picture is different, so check with your tax professional before you plan around any of this.

Home prices

The Southern Nevada median single-family price was $475,000 in October 2026 (Las Vegas REALTORS, as reported by FOX5). Tell me your budget, and I'll show you what it buys here.

Getting back and forth

Southwest lists nonstop flights from Portland to Las Vegas at about 2 hours 9 minutes. Henderson and Seven Hills are about 20 minutes from the Las Vegas airport.

Questions people ask

Do I save on taxes moving from Portland to Las Vegas?

It depends on your income and spending. Nevada has no state income tax, while Oregon's top rate is 9.9%. But Oregon has no sales tax, and Clark County's is 8.375%. Oregon also has an estate tax on estates of $1 million or more, and Nevada does not. Check with your tax professional.

How different is the weather?

Very. NOAA normals show about 37 inches of rain a year at Portland's airport and about 4 inches in Las Vegas, with hot summers here.

How long is the flight?

Southwest lists nonstop flights from Portland to Las Vegas at about 2 hours 9 minutes.

Can I buy without flying in?

Yes. Nevada allows electronic signatures and remote online notarization, your agent can tour homes for you on video, and a licensed home inspector you hire inspects the home.
