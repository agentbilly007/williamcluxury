Condo or condo-hotel: the first question

A condo is a home you own and can live in full time, like any other home. A condo-hotel unit sits inside a working hotel. Owners can usually stay in it, and many put it in the hotel's rental program when they're not using it. The hotel's rules set how that works, so read them before you write an offer.

On November 21, 2026, 237 of the 529 units for sale were in the condo-hotel buildings on this page (Trump International Hotel & Tower Las Vegas, Vdara Hotel & Spa, Palms Place, The Signature at MGM Grand).

Financing is the other big difference. Many lenders treat condo-hotels differently than regular condos, so cash or specialty loans are common. If you plan to use a loan, talk to a lender about the specific building before you pick a unit.

What to check before you buy any high-rise unit

  • The HOA and what it covers. Fees in these buildings often include things a house wouldn't, like concierge, security, cable or internet. Ask for the budget and reserve study.
  • Rental rules. Each building sets its own rules on short-term and long-term rentals. Get them in writing.
  • The view, floor by floor. Two units on the same floor can face opposite ways. I check the direction and the floor for every unit we look at.
  • Parking and storage. Some units come with assigned spaces, some use valet, some have storage, some don't.
