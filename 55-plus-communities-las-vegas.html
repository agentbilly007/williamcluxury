How they differ

  • Size: Sun City Summerlin has 7,779 homes and Sun City Anthem has 7,144, while Solera at Anthem has 1,822 and Siena has 2,001, according to their associations and the Review-Journal.
  • Age of the homes: Sun City Summerlin was built from 1989 to 1999, the oldest of the six. Solera and Sun City Aliante were built in the 2000s.
  • Gates: Siena is guard-gated, according to its association. Sun City Anthem has one gated neighborhood, Pinnacle Village, and Solera has the gated Villas at Solera section.
  • Golf: Sun City Summerlin's association lists three golf courses. Siena Golf Course runs through Siena, Desert Willow is part of Sun City MacDonald Ranch, and a city-owned course runs through Sun City Aliante.

HOAs

Each of these communities has its own HOA, and fees can be different from one street to the next, so I don't quote one number. I pull the exact fees for any home you're serious about and go through the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Questions people ask

What are the 55+ communities in Las Vegas and Henderson?

This guide compares six age-qualified communities: Sun City Summerlin and Siena in Summerlin, Sun City Anthem, Solera at Anthem and Sun City MacDonald Ranch in Henderson, and Sun City Aliante in North Las Vegas.

How much do homes cost in Las Vegas 55+ communities?

On November 16, 2026, the median asking price for single-family homes ranged from $379,900 in Sun City Aliante to $775,000 in Siena. Prices change every week, so ask me for today's numbers.

Do I have to be 55 to buy?

Age rules are set by each community's HOA. For example, Sun City Summerlin's policy requires at least one resident age 55 or older in each home. Read the current rules in the HOA documents for any home you consider.
