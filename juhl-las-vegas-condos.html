What Juhl is

Juhl opened in June 2009 as a set of connected residential buildings, the tallest 15 stories. It has about 340 lofts and condos, with shops and restaurants at street level. These are full-time condos, not hotel units.

What's for sale today

On November 21, 2026, 29 units were for sale at Juhl (1 studio, 20 one-bedroom, 8 two-bedroom), from $159,900 to $675,555. The median asking price was $319,900. Listed floors ran from 1 to 11.

HOA fees

On the 29 active listings, the total monthly HOA (all associations combined) ran from $638 to $851, with a median of $698. Studios: median $638. One-bedrooms: median $689. Two-bedrooms: median $749. Listings most often show the fee covering concierge, building management, water and elevators. Ask for the current budget, the reserve study and what's included for the exact unit.

Views and amenities

Views depend on the floor and which way the unit faces. On the 29 active listings with views entered, listing agents marked views of the city (27), the mountains (13), the Strip (6) and the pool (1). Many units list more than one. I check the direction and the floor for every unit we look at.

Building features most often listed in the MLS for Juhl: pool, fitness center, barbecue area, concierge, exercise room, pool cabanas, security and spa. Confirm what is open to owners for the unit you are buying.

Rental rules

Rental rules at Juhl are set by the HOA, including any limits on short-term rentals and minimum lease terms. Local short-term rental rules also apply. Read the CC&Rs and the current rules before you buy if you might rent the unit out.

On November 21, 2026, 3 of the 29 active listings were offered with furniture included.

Financing

Lenders review the building as well as the buyer for a condo loan, for example the HOA budget and reserves. If you plan to use a loan, ask your lender to look at this building early. I can share names of local lenders, and you choose.

Location

Juhl sits at 353 E Bonneville Ave, 89101, in downtown Las Vegas. The Arts District and Fremont Street are close by, and I-15 and US 95 meet nearby.
