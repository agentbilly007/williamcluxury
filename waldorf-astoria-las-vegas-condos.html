What Waldorf Astoria Las Vegas Residences is

The tower opened in December 2009 as the Mandarin Oriental, Las Vegas, and was renamed Waldorf Astoria Las Vegas in August 2018. It is 47 stories, with the hotel on the lower floors and 225 condo residences. Residents and hotel guests use separate entrances.

The residences are individually owned condos. Rules on leasing are set by the HOA, so read the CC&Rs before you buy if you might rent the unit out.

Views and amenities

Views depend on the floor and which way the unit faces. On the 11 active listings with views entered, listing agents marked views of the city (11), the mountains (9) and the Strip (9). Many units list more than one. I check the direction and the floor for every unit we look at.

Building features most often listed in the MLS for Waldorf Astoria Las Vegas Residences: fitness center, security, concierge, pool, spa, pool cabanas, pet park and business center. Confirm what is open to owners for the unit you are buying.

Rental rules

Rental rules at Waldorf Astoria Las Vegas Residences are set by the HOA, including any limits on short-term rentals and minimum lease terms. Local short-term rental rules also apply. Read the CC&Rs and the current rules before you buy if you might rent the unit out.

On November 21, 2026, 2 of the 11 active listings were offered with furniture included.

Financing

Lenders review the building as well as the buyer for a condo loan, for example the HOA budget and reserves. If you plan to use a loan, ask your lender to look at this building early. I can share names of local lenders, and you choose.
