Who I help

  • Retirees moving here from another state. A lot of the work happens before you ever fly in: we narrow the areas, I tour homes for you on video, and you visit only if and when you want to.
  • Buyers coming from California. I know the questions you'll have about taxes, Prop 13 and HOAs here, and I point you to the official sources. Read retiring to Las Vegas from California.
  • Buyers comparing 55+ communities. Sun City Summerlin, Siena, Sun City Anthem, Solera, Sun City MacDonald Ranch and Sun City Aliante each have a full guide on this site, with prices and recent sales. See them side by side.
  • Buyers downsizing. Fewer rooms, no stairs, a smaller yard, a casita for visiting family. Tell me the list and I search for exactly that.

I help every kind of buyer, of any age. This page just explains how I work with people who are retiring or looking at 55+ communities.

What I do for you

  • Live video tours. I tour homes for you on FaceTime or send a full video walkthrough: every room, the backyard, the view and the street. My home tour videos are on YouTube and Instagram, so you can see how I film before we start.
  • Single-story search. I set up a search for single-story homes in the areas you pick, inside or outside a 55+ community. See single-story homes in 55+ communities.
  • 55+ community comparisons. Location, size, age of the homes, gates and golf, with the HOA's own description of its age rules.
  • HOA fees and documents. I pull the current HOA fees and the resale package for any home you're serious about. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).
  • The real tax bill. I show you the current property tax bill on a home, and remind you to claim the 3% primary residence cap after closing. See Las Vegas property tax.
  • Drive times that matter to you. When we shortlist homes, I show you the drive to the hospital, doctors, golf course or family you'd visit most.
  • Working with your lender and escrow. I can share names of local lenders, and you choose. Paying cash? We line up proof of funds. I stay in touch with your lender and your escrow officer through closing.
  • Inspection access. A licensed home inspector you hire does the inspection and writes the report. I can give the inspector access and, with their OK, send you video.

Buying from out of state, step by step

Nevada allows electronic signatures (NRS 719.240) and remote online notarization (NRS 240.181 to 240.206), so you can sign from home. Your escrow officer coordinates the closing signature with you. Your timeline depends on your loan and your contract. The full process is on buying a Las Vegas home sight unseen.
