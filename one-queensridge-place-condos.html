What One Queensridge Place is

One Queensridge Place is two 20-story towers that were finished in 2007, with 219 condos in all. Each tower is topped by two-story penthouses. These are full-time condos, not hotel units, and they sit inside the Queensridge area off Rampart Blvd.

What's for sale today

On November 21, 2026, 12 units were for sale at One Queensridge Place (6 two-bedroom, 4 three-bedroom, 2 four-bedroom), from $945,000 to $5,500,000. The median asking price was $1,622,000. Listed floors ran from 0 to 17.

HOA fees

Views and amenities

Views depend on the floor and which way the unit faces. On the 12 active listings with views entered, listing agents marked views of the city (11), the mountains (11), the Strip (5), a golf course (2), the pool (2) and a courtyard (1). Many units list more than one. I check the direction and the floor for every unit we look at.

Building features most often listed in the MLS for One Queensridge Place: wine lockers, fitness center, indoor pool, guard-gated entry, indoor spa, theater room, security and pet park. Confirm what is open to owners for the unit you are buying.

Rental rules

Rental rules at One Queensridge Place are set by the HOA, including any limits on short-term rentals and minimum lease terms. Local short-term rental rules also apply. Read the CC&Rs and the current rules before you buy if you might rent the unit out.

Financing

Lenders review the building as well as the buyer for a condo loan, for example the HOA budget and reserves. If you plan to use a loan, ask your lender to look at this building early. I can share names of local lenders, and you choose.

Location

The towers sit at 9101 and 9103 Alta Dr, 89145, near Rampart Blvd and Charleston Blvd on the west side. Downtown Summerlin is a short drive northwest. See the Queensridge guide for the homes around it.
