About this home

A renovated single-story end unit in gated Sunrise Villas, three miles from the Las Vegas Strip. At 1,735 sq ft this is the largest of the standard floor plans in the community, and the end-unit position means more light and only one shared wall.

The center of the home is a glass atrium — an open-air courtyard the living space wraps around, so daylight reaches rooms that would otherwise sit interior. Vaulted ceilings and a gas fireplace anchor the living room.

Renovated throughout: quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, raised-panel cabinetry and new carpet. Newer A/C unit. Two-car attached garage, tile roof, and a private walled patio off the living area.

Sunrise Villas is gated with a community pool, spa and tennis courts. Everything is single level — no stairs anywhere in the home.
