What I do for California buyers

  • Narrow Henderson down. Green Valley, Seven Hills, Anthem, Inspirada, Cadence, MacDonald Highlands and Lake Las Vegas are very different. I match them to your budget, commute and the kind of street you want. Start with the Henderson guide or Summerlin vs Henderson.
  • Tour homes for you on video. Live on FaceTime, or a full walkthrough you can share with family. See how my video tours work and my tours by area.
  • Show you the real monthly cost. I pull the current property tax bill and HOA fees on any home you're serious about. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive the HOA resale package (NRS 116.4109).
  • Point you to the tax facts. Nevada has no state personal income tax, and Clark County's sales tax is 8.375%. Prop 13 and Prop 19 stay in California. Details are in retiring to Las Vegas from California and Las Vegas property tax. Taxes depend on your situation, so check with your tax professional.
  • Work with your lender and escrow. I can share names of local lenders, and you choose. Paying cash? We line up proof of funds.
  • Inspection access. A licensed home inspector you hire does the inspection and writes the report. I can give the inspector access and, with their OK, send you video.

Selling in California

I'm licensed in Nevada and handle the Henderson side. For your California sale, work with a California-licensed agent there, and ask your CPA about the tax on the sale and California's withholding rules.

Buying before you move

Nevada allows electronic signatures (NRS 719.240) and remote online notarization (NRS 240.181 to 240.206), and your escrow officer coordinates the signing. Your timeline depends on your loan and your contract. Step by step: buying a Las Vegas home sight unseen.
