What Turnberry Towers is

Turnberry Towers is two 45-story towers built by the same developer as Turnberry Place. The East tower was completed in 2007 and the West tower in 2008, with 318 units each. Each tower has its own pool and gym. These are full-time condos, not hotel units.

What's for sale today

On November 21, 2026, 39 units were for sale at Turnberry Towers (8 one-bedroom, 30 two-bedroom, 1 three-bedroom), from $320,000 to $925,000. The median asking price was $660,000. Listed floors ran from 0 to 308.

HOA fees

On the 39 active listings, the total monthly HOA (all associations combined) ran from $650 to $1,802, with a median of $1,029. One-bedrooms: median $674. Two-bedrooms: median $1,032. Three-bedrooms: median $1,173. Listings most often show the fee covering concierge, building management and gated entry. Views and amenities

Views depend on the floor and which way the unit faces. On the 39 active listings with views entered, listing agents marked views of the city (36), the mountains (26), the Strip (22), a golf course (18), the pool (10) and a lake or water feature (4). Many units list more than one. I check the direction and the floor for every unit we look at.

Building features most often listed in the MLS for Turnberry Towers: pool, fitness center, guard-gated entry, barbecue area, concierge, tennis courts, spa and pet park. Confirm what is open to owners for the unit you are buying.

Rental rules

Rental rules at Turnberry Towers are set by the HOA, including any limits on short-term rentals and minimum lease terms. Local short-term rental rules also apply. Read the CC&Rs and the current rules before you buy if you might rent the unit out.

On November 21, 2026, 5 of the 39 active listings were offered with furniture included.

Financing

Lenders review the building as well as the buyer for a condo loan, for example the HOA budget and reserves. If you plan to use a loan, ask your lender to look at this building early. I can share names of local lenders, and you choose.

Location

The towers sit on Karen Ave, 89109, just east of the north end of the Strip and near the Convention Center. The Las Vegas Country Club is a short walk east.
