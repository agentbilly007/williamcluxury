Many San Diego and Orange County buyers come to Las Vegas for a bigger home, a lower price, and no state income tax, while staying close enough to drive back to the coast for a weekend. Here's the side-by-side, including the parts that don't change as much as people expect.

Home prices

In September 2026, the median sold price for an existing single-family home was $1,050,000 in San Diego County and $1,480,000 in Orange County (California Association of REALTORS). In Southern Nevada, the median single-family price was $475,000 in October 2026 (Las Vegas REALTORS, as reported by FOX5). Medians hide a lot of variety, so I compare the specific neighborhoods you'd actually live in.

Income tax

Nevada does not tax personal income. California's top income tax rate is 12.3%, plus an extra 1% on income over $1 million (tax year 2025, California Franchise Tax Board). Your savings depend on your situation, so talk with your tax professional.

Sales tax: closer than you'd think

Clark County's sales tax rate is 8.375%. The City of San Diego, Irvine and Anaheim are 7.75%, and Santa Ana is 9.25% (California Department of Tax and Fee Administration, rates effective October 1, 2026). So depending on where you live now, your sales tax may go up a little, not down.

Prop 19 stays in California

If you're 55 or older, you may have heard you can take your low Prop 13 tax base with you when you move. Under Prop 19, that transfer works only for a replacement home in California (California State Board of Equalization). It doesn't carry over to Nevada. Here, the tax bill on your primary residence can't go up more than 3% a year (Clark County), which is a different kind of protection. When we look at a home, I show you its current tax bill.

The drive up I-15

San Diego to Las Vegas is about 330 miles, roughly five hours on I-15 (Enterprise travel guide). From Orange County it's a bit shorter. That makes it easy to keep weekend trips back to the coast on the calendar.

You won't be the only one

About 53,300 people moved from California to Nevada in 2024, the most recent year in the Census Bureau's state-to-state migration data.

Questions people ask

Is Las Vegas cheaper than San Diego or Orange County?

On home prices, generally yes. In September 2026 the single-family median was $1,050,000 in San Diego County and $1,480,000 in Orange County (C.A.R.), and the Southern Nevada median was $475,000 in October 2026.

Is sales tax lower in Las Vegas?

Not always. Clark County is 8.375%. San Diego, Irvine and Anaheim are 7.75%, and Santa Ana is 9.25% (CDTFA, October 2026).

Can I transfer my Prop 13 tax base to Nevada?

No. Prop 19 base-year transfers apply only to a replacement home in California. In Nevada, the tax bill on a primary residence is capped at a 3% increase per year.

How far is the drive from San Diego?

About 330 miles, roughly five hours on I-15.
