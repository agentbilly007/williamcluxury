Retirees moving to Las Vegas or Henderson usually choose between two kinds of places: a 55+ community with a clubhouse and activities, or a single-story home in a regular neighborhood. Both are easy to find here. This guide covers taxes, the 55+ communities, single-story homes, health care, weather and how to buy from out of state. Taxes in retirement

  • Income tax: Nevada's constitution says no income tax may be levied on the wages or personal income of individuals. That means no state income tax on Social Security, pensions, IRA or 401(k) withdrawals. Federal taxes still apply.
  • Inheritance and estate tax: The Nevada Constitution bans inheritance tax, and Nevada has not required an estate tax filing for deaths on or after January 1, 2005 (Nevada Department of Taxation).
  • Property tax: On your primary residence, the yearly increase in your tax bill is capped at 3% (NRS 361.4723). After you buy, you claim the cap by returning the Assessor's notice. My Las Vegas property tax guide walks through rates, due dates and the cap.
  • Sales tax: 8.375% in Clark County.

This is general information, not tax advice. Run your own numbers with your CPA or tax professional before you decide. Coming from California? Read retiring to Las Vegas from California for a side-by-side.

Property tax exemptions to ask about

The Clark County Assessor offers exemptions for veterans, disabled veterans (60% or more service-connected disability), surviving spouses, and people who are legally blind, each with its own rules. The county does not list a general senior exemption. After you buy, I'll point you to the Assessor's page so you can apply for any that fit.

55+ communities

Federal law lets a community call itself 55+ when at least 80% of its occupied homes have at least one resident 55 or older (24 CFR 100.305). Each community's HOA sets its own age and occupancy rules. These six have full guides on this site, with homes for sale, prices and recent sales:

  • Sun City Summerlin (Summerlin, Las Vegas): the oldest and largest, built from 1989 to 1999
  • Siena (Summerlin South): guard-gated, with Siena Golf Course
  • Sun City Anthem (Henderson): a large 55+ community in the Anthem hills
  • Solera at Anthem (Henderson): smaller, built in the 2000s
  • Sun City MacDonald Ranch (Henderson): home to Desert Willow golf course
  • Sun City Aliante (North Las Vegas): built in the 2000s

Newer 55+ choices include Del Webb at Lake Las Vegas in Henderson, Heritage at Stonebridge in Summerlin and Del Webb at North Ranch in North Las Vegas. See all six older communities compared side by side.

Each one has its own HOA, amenities and dues. I pull the current fees and the HOA resale package for any home you're serious about, and in Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Health care nearby

Hospitals in the areas retirees often look at include Henderson Hospital and Dignity Health St. Rose Dominican, Siena Campus in Henderson, Summerlin Hospital Medical Center in Summerlin, and University Medical Center (UMC) in central Las Vegas. Veterans are served by the VA Southern Nevada Healthcare System, whose main medical center is in North Las Vegas. When we shortlist homes, I'll show you the drive time to the ones you'd use.

On Medicare Advantage or a drug plan? If you move out of your plan's service area, Medicare gives you a window to switch that starts when you move and lasts two full months after (Medicare.gov). Check plans for your new ZIP code before you move.

Weather

Las Vegas gets about 4.18 inches of rain in a normal year. The normal high is 104.5°F in July and 58.5°F in January, with January lows near 40.5°F (National Weather Service, 1991-2020 normals at Harry Reid International Airport). Summer afternoons are hot, so when we tour I'll point out covered patios, shade and which way the backyard faces.

Buying from out of state

Many retirees buy before they move. Nevada allows electronic signatures (NRS 719.240) and remote online notarization (NRS 240.181 to 240.206), and I tour homes for you live on FaceTime or by full video walkthrough. Questions people ask

Does Nevada tax Social Security or pensions?

No. Nevada has no personal income tax, so there is no state income tax on Social Security, pensions or retirement account withdrawals. Federal tax still applies. Check with your tax professional.

Does Nevada have an estate or inheritance tax?

The Nevada Constitution bans inheritance tax, and Nevada has not required an estate tax filing for deaths on or after January 1, 2005.

Is there a senior property tax exemption in Clark County?

The Clark County Assessor lists exemptions for veterans, disabled veterans, surviving spouses and the blind, but not a general senior exemption. Your primary residence's tax bill increase is capped at 3% a year.

What are the 55+ communities in Las Vegas and Henderson?

Sun City Summerlin and Siena in Summerlin, Sun City Anthem, Solera at Anthem and Sun City MacDonald Ranch in Henderson, and Sun City Aliante in North Las Vegas, plus newer ones such as Del Webb at Lake Las Vegas, Heritage at Stonebridge and Del Webb at North Ranch.

What makes a community 55+?

Under federal rules, at least 80% of the occupied homes must have at least one resident 55 or older. Each community's own HOA rules can add more.

How hot does it get in Las Vegas?

The normal July high is 104.5°F and the normal January high is 58.5°F, based on National Weather Service 1991-2020 normals. Rain averages about 4.18 inches a year.
