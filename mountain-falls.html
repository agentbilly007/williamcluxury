About Mountain Falls

Mountain Falls is a golf course community on the southern edge of Pahrump in Nye County. Its public 18-hole, par-72 course at 5001 Fox Avenue opened in 2002, with a design credited to Cal Olson and Jack Nicklaus.

Ovation, a gated neighborhood inside Mountain Falls, opened in 2018 with about 600 homes planned and its own residents-only clubhouse, and its residents can also use the Mountain Falls clubhouse. Taylor Morrison lists newer neighborhoods there today.

Age-qualified community (55+). Ovation, one neighborhood inside Mountain Falls, is a 55-plus age-qualified community, according to the Pahrump Valley Times. The rest of Mountain Falls is not described as age-qualified. Age rules are set by the HOA, so read the current rules in the HOA documents for any home you consider.

HOAs

HOA fees can be different from one street to the next, so I don't quote one number. I pull the exact fees for any home you're serious about and go through the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Schools

Mountain Falls is in the Nye County School District, which assigns schools by street address. Check the zoning for any address with the Nye County School District before you write an offer. I'll help you look it up.
