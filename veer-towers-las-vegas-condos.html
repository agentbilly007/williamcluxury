What Veer Towers is

Veer Towers is two 37-story glass towers that lean in opposite directions, part of the CityCenter development on the Strip. They opened in July 2010 with 670 condo units in all. There is no hotel or casino in the towers. These are condos you own and can live in full time.

What's for sale today

On November 21, 2026, 33 units were for sale at Veer Towers (7 studios, 13 one-bedroom, 13 two-bedroom), from $279,000 to $5,595,000. The median asking price was $600,000. Listed floors ran from 3 to 36.

HOA fees

On the 32 active listings, the total monthly HOA (all associations combined) ran from $577 to $3,602, with a median of $1,120. Studios: median $870. One-bedrooms: median $1,075. Two-bedrooms: median $1,708. Listings most often show the fee covering building management and concierge. Ask for the current budget, the reserve study and what's included for the exact unit.

Views and amenities

Views depend on the floor and which way the unit faces. On the 33 active listings with views entered, listing agents marked views of the Strip (25), the city (23), the mountains (14) and the pool (1). Many units list more than one. I check the direction and the floor for every unit we look at.

Building features most often listed in the MLS for Veer Towers: pool, concierge, fitness center, security, spa and business center. Confirm what is open to owners for the unit you are buying.

Rental rules

Rental rules at Veer Towers are set by the HOA, including any limits on short-term rentals and minimum lease terms. Local short-term rental rules also apply. Read the CC&Rs and the current rules before you buy if you might rent the unit out.

On November 21, 2026, 14 of the 33 active listings were offered with furniture included.

Financing

Lenders review the building as well as the buyer for a condo loan, for example the HOA budget and reserves. If you plan to use a loan, ask your lender to look at this building early. I can share names of local lenders, and you choose.

Location

The towers sit on Las Vegas Blvd at CityCenter, 89158, next to Aria and the Waldorf Astoria. Vdara and Bellagio are a short walk away, and I-15 is just west.
