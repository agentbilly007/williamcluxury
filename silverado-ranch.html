About Silverado Ranch

Silverado Ranch is in the southeast part of the Las Vegas valley, around Silverado Ranch Boulevard. The South Point Hotel, Casino & Spa, at Las Vegas Boulevard and Silverado Ranch Boulevard next to I-15, opened in 2005.

Clark County's Silverado Ranch Park has baseball and softball fields, a skate park, dog park areas, a walking trail and a playground. Next door, the 40,000 square foot Silverado Ranch Community Center opened in 2024 with an indoor walking track, a demonstration kitchen and a gym for pickleball, basketball and volleyball.

HOAs

HOA fees can be different from one street to the next, so I don't quote one number. I pull the exact fees for any home you're serious about and go through the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Schools

Silverado Ranch is in the Clark County School District, which assigns schools by street address. Check the zoning for any address with the Clark County School District before you write an offer. I'll help you look it up.
