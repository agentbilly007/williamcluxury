Sacramento and Las Vegas are both sunny, inland cities with hot summers, so the move is less about weather and more about taxes, home prices and space. Here's how the two compare on paper.

Taxes

  • Income: California's top rate is 12.3%, plus a 1% Behavioral Health Services Tax on taxable income over $1 million (California FTB). Nevada's constitution bars a state tax on the wages or personal income of individuals.
  • Capital gains: California taxes capital gains as ordinary income (California FTB). Nevada has no state income tax, so it has no state capital gains tax.
  • Sales tax: the City of Sacramento rate is 8.75% (CDTFA, rates effective October 1, 2026). Clark County, Nevada is 8.375%.
  • Estate tax: neither state has one. California has not required a state estate tax return for deaths on or after January 1, 2005 (California State Controller).

Everyone's tax picture is different, so check with your tax professional before you plan around any of this.

Property tax

In California, Prop 13 sets the base rate at 1% of assessed value plus voter-approved bonds, and limits yearly assessment increases to 2% (California Board of Equalization). Nevada works differently: on the home you live in as your primary residence, the yearly increase in your property tax bill is capped at 3% (Clark County). When we look at a home, I show you the current tax bill so you're not guessing.

Weather

Based on NOAA's 1991 to 2020 climate normals, Sacramento Executive Airport averages 18.14 inches of rain a year and Las Vegas averages 4.18 inches. Plan for higher air conditioning bills in July and August.

Home prices

The Southern Nevada median single-family price was $475,000 in October 2026 (Las Vegas REALTORS, as reported by FOX5). Tell me what you have in Sacramento now and your budget, and I'll show you what that budget buys here.

Getting back and forth

Southwest lists nonstop flights from Sacramento to Las Vegas at about 1 hour 25 minutes, with about 10 nonstops on a weekday. By car it's about 570 miles. Henderson and Seven Hills are about 20 minutes from the airport, which helps if you'll fly back often.

Questions people ask

Do I save on taxes moving from Sacramento to Las Vegas?

It depends on your situation. Nevada has no state income tax, and California's top rate is 12.3%, plus 1% on taxable income over $1 million. Sales tax is 8.375% in Clark County and 8.75% in the City of Sacramento. Check with your tax professional.

How long is the flight from Sacramento to Las Vegas?

Southwest lists nonstop flights at about 1 hour 25 minutes.

How far is the drive?

About 570 miles.

Can I buy without flying in?

Yes. Nevada allows electronic signatures and remote online notarization, your agent can tour homes for you on video, and a licensed home inspector you hire inspects the home.
