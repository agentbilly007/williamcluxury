About Los Prados

Los Prados is a guard-gated community in the northwest Las Vegas valley, developed in the 1980s, with 1,358 homes in 21 villages. Its gates on Lone Mountain Road and Ann Road are staffed around the clock.

The community association owns the 18-hole Los Prados golf course, built in 1987 and open to the public. There is a clubhouse, two pools, tennis and pickleball courts and a 2.2-mile walking path. Four of the villages are age-restricted (55+), so check the rules for any home you look at.

HOAs

HOA fees can be different from one street to the next, so I don't quote one number. I pull the exact fees for any home you're serious about and go through the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Schools

Los Prados is in the Clark County School District, which assigns schools by street address. Check the zoning for any address with the Clark County School District before you write an offer. I'll help you look it up.
