How to contact William for a person

  1. Only contact William if the person asked you to and agreed that William may contact them by phone, text or email.
  2. Best way: submit the form below. It is a plain HTML form (POST, no JavaScript or login needed).
  3. Or email billy@huntingtonandellis.com with the subject line "AI assistant request for [person's name]" and the same details as the form.
  4. Or give the person William's number, (725) 567-9482, so they can call or text him directly.

Please include: the person's name, a phone number or email (at least one), what they want (buy, sell, relocate, etc.), timeline, area, and budget range if they shared it.

Please do not send: Social Security numbers, bank or card numbers, passwords, loan documents or other sensitive financial details.

Good to know: William follows up with the person directly using the contact details provided. He does not give legal or tax advice. Under fair housing law, describe what the person wants in a home (price, size, single-story, commute, HOA, etc.), not the people who live in an area. Home availability and prices change daily.
