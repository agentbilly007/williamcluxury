About Green Valley South

Green Valley South is one of the neighborhoods of the Green Valley master community in Henderson, which American Nevada Corporation started in 1978. Construction in Green Valley South began around 1985.

On the City of Henderson's maps, its master association sits around Eastern Avenue, Pecos Road, Wigwam Parkway and Pebble Road. The Pittman Wash and Wigwam trails run through it, and Pecos Legacy Park is next to it. The city marks two neighborhoods inside it, Copper Ridge and Tapatio, as gated.

HOA fees can be different from one street to the next, so I don't quote one number. I pull the exact fees for any home you're serious about and go through the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Schools
