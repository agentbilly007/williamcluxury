What The Signature at MGM Grand is

The Signature at MGM Grand is three 38-story towers with 576 units each, 1,728 in all. Tower 1 opened in May 2006 and Tower 2 later that year, with Tower 3 scheduled for 2007. The towers are non-gaming and non-smoking, with their own entrance and staff, and each connects to MGM Grand by a walkway.

What's for sale today

On November 21, 2026, 98 units were for sale at The Signature at MGM Grand (51 studios, 38 one-bedroom, 9 two-bedroom), from $225,000 to $830,000. The median asking price was $322,500. Listed floors ran from 2 to 38.

HOA fees

On the 98 active listings, the total monthly HOA (all associations combined) ran from $902 to $4,072, with a median of $998. Studios: median $991. One-bedrooms: median $1,966. Two-bedrooms: median $2,972. Listings most often show the fee covering concierge, water, sewer, recreation areas, gated entry, grounds upkeep and trash. Views and amenities

Views depend on the floor and which way the unit faces. On the 98 active listings with views entered, listing agents marked views of the city (66), the mountains (63), the Strip (42), a golf course (27), the pool (24), a courtyard (1) and a park or greenbelt (1). Many units list more than one. I check the direction and the floor for every unit we look at.

Building features most often listed in the MLS for The Signature at MGM Grand: guard-gated entry, pool, business center, fitness center, concierge, pool cabanas, security and exercise room. Confirm what is open to owners for the unit you are buying.

Using the unit and renting it out

Owners at The Signature at MGM Grand can usually stay in their unit, and many place it in the hotel's rental program when they're not using it. On November 21, 2026, 68 of the 98 active listings mentioned a rental program in the listing remarks, and 96 were listed with furniture included. How the program works, its fees and how revenue is shared are set by the hotel program and the HOA, and they can change. Rental income is never certain. Read the rental program agreement and the CC&Rs before you write an offer.

Financing a condo-hotel

Many lenders treat condo-hotels differently than regular condos, so cash or specialty loans are common. If you plan to use a loan, talk to a lender about this building before you pick a unit, and budget extra time.

Location

The towers sit on E Harmon Ave, 89109, just east of the Strip behind MGM Grand. The Strip is a short walk west.
