What Trump Tower Las Vegas is

Trump International Hotel & Tower Las Vegas is a 64-story glass tower at 2000 Fashion Show Dr, just west of the Strip. It opened in 2008 with about 1,282 units. It's a condo-hotel: each unit is individually owned, but the building runs as a hotel, and the hotel describes itself as non-gaming and non-smoking.

Owners can stay in their unit, and many put it in the hotel's rental program when they're not using it. In the MLS on November 21, 2026, 65 of the 67 active listings mentioned the rental program in the listing remarks, and 66 were listed with furniture included. The hotel's rules set how the program works, so read them before you write an offer.

HOA fees

On the 67 active listings, the total monthly HOA (all associations combined) ran from $623 to $1,324, with a median of $661. Studios: median $661. One-bedrooms: median $1,323. Listings commonly show the fee covering items like cable, internet, water, cleaning, concierge and building management. Ask for the current budget and what's included for the exact unit.

Views

Views depend on the floor and which way the unit faces. On the 67 active listings with views entered, listing agents marked views of the Strip (40), the city (39), the mountains (29) (many units list more than one). I check the direction and the floor for every unit we look at.

Hotel amenities

Per the hotel's own website: a pool with skyline views and poolside cabanas, The Spa at Trump, a fitness center, DJT restaurant, H2(EAU) poolside dining and in-room dining. MLS listings for the building also note concierge, valet, 24-hour security and a business center. Hotel services and what owners can use are set by the hotel and can change, so confirm them for the unit you're buying.

Location

The tower sits at 2000 Fashion Show Dr, 89109, behind Fashion Show mall on the west side of the Strip. The hotel lists the Las Vegas Convention Center and the Sphere as a few minutes away. I-15 is close by for trips to the airport, Summerlin or Henderson.

Financing a condo-hotel

Many lenders treat condo-hotels differently than regular condos, so cash or specialty loans are common. If you plan to use a loan, talk to a lender about this building before you pick a unit, and budget extra time.
