About Las Vegas Country Club

The Las Vegas Country Club is a private club in the Winchester area of central Las Vegas, built on the site of a 1950s racetrack. Its 18-hole course was finished in 1967 and its 44,000 square foot clubhouse in 1968.

The club has hosted the Las Vegas Invitational, the LPGA Takefuji Classic and, since 2024, LIV Golf Las Vegas. It offers golf, racquet sports, fitness and dining. Membership is separate from owning a home nearby.

HOAs

HOA fees can be different from one street to the next, so I don't quote one number. I pull the exact fees for any home you're serious about and go through the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Schools

Las Vegas Country Club is in the Clark County School District, which assigns schools by street address. Check the zoning for any address with the Clark County School District before you write an offer. I'll help you look it up.
