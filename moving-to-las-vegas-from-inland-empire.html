Moving to Las Vegas
from the Inland Empire.

From Riverside or San Bernardino, Las Vegas is about a 230-mile drive up I-15. Here's how the two compare on taxes, weather and getting back and forth.

The Inland Empire is close enough that many people who move to Las Vegas still drive back for family and work. The weather is similar, so the biggest differences are taxes, home prices and the kind of home you can get for your budget.

The drive

Riverside to Las Vegas is about 235 miles, and San Bernardino is about 225 miles, mostly on I-15. If you'd rather fly, Southwest lists nonstop flights from Ontario Airport to Las Vegas at about 1 hour 5 minutes.

Taxes

  • Income: California's top rate is 12.3%, plus a 1% Behavioral Health Services Tax on taxable income over $1 million (California FTB). Nevada's constitution bars a state tax on the wages or personal income of individuals.
  • Capital gains: California taxes capital gains as ordinary income (California FTB). Nevada has no state income tax, so it has no state capital gains tax.
  • Sales tax: 8.75% in the cities of Riverside and San Bernardino (CDTFA). Clark County, Nevada is 8.375%.

Everyone's tax picture is different, so check with your tax professional before you plan around any of this.

Property tax

In California, Prop 13 sets the base rate at 1% of assessed value plus voter-approved bonds, and limits yearly assessment increases to 2% (California Board of Equalization). In Nevada, on the home you live in as your primary residence, the yearly increase in your property tax bill is capped at 3% (Clark County). When we look at a home, I show you the current tax bill.

Weather

Based on NOAA's 1991 to 2020 climate normals, March Air Reserve Base near Riverside averages 10.65 inches of rain a year and Las Vegas averages 4.18 inches. Both have hot summers.

Home prices

The Southern Nevada median single-family price was $475,000 in October 2026 (Las Vegas REALTORS, as reported by FOX5). Tell me your budget, and I'll show you what it buys here, from homes under $500,000 in Henderson to homes with pools in the southwest valley.

Where Inland Empire buyers tend to look

  • Mountain's Edge and Southern Highlands, on the south end of the valley
  • Henderson, for quick airport access
  • Summerlin, for parks and Red Rock Canyon

Questions people ask

How far is Las Vegas from Riverside?

About 235 miles by car, mostly on I-15. San Bernardino is about 225 miles.

Do I save on taxes moving from the Inland Empire to Las Vegas?

It depends on your situation. Nevada has no state income tax, and California's top rate is 12.3%, plus 1% on taxable income over $1 million. Sales tax is 8.375% in Clark County and 8.75% in Riverside and San Bernardino. Check with your tax professional.

Is there a quick flight?

Southwest lists nonstop flights from Ontario Airport to Las Vegas at about 1 hour 5 minutes.

Can I buy without moving first?

Yes. Nevada allows electronic signatures and remote online notarization, your agent can tour homes for you on video, and a licensed home inspector you hire inspects the home.
