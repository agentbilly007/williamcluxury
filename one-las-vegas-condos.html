What One Las Vegas is

One Las Vegas opened in June 2008 as two 20-story towers with 359 condos. It sits on Las Vegas Blvd well south of the resort corridor. These are full-time condos with no hotel in the building.

What's for sale today

On November 21, 2026, 20 units were for sale at One Las Vegas (1 one-bedroom, 15 two-bedroom, 3 three-bedroom, 1 four-bedroom), from $299,000 to $749,900. The median asking price was $410,000. Listed floors ran from 2 to 19.

HOA fees

On the 20 active listings, the total monthly HOA (all associations combined) ran from $654 to $2,066, with a median of $1,038. One-bedrooms: median $654. Two-bedrooms: median $1,037. Three-bedrooms: median $1,587. Four-bedrooms: median $2,066. Listings most often show the fee covering concierge. Views and amenities

Views depend on the floor and which way the unit faces. On the 20 active listings with views entered, listing agents marked views of the mountains (17), the city (13), the Strip (7), the pool (3) and a courtyard (1). Many units list more than one. I check the direction and the floor for every unit we look at.

Building features most often listed in the MLS for One Las Vegas: pool, barbecue area, fitness center, pet park, theater room, business center, concierge and tennis courts. Confirm what is open to owners for the unit you are buying.

Rental rules

Rental rules at One Las Vegas are set by the HOA, including any limits on short-term rentals and minimum lease terms. Local short-term rental rules also apply. Read the CC&Rs and the current rules before you buy if you might rent the unit out.

On November 21, 2026, 1 of the 20 active listings were offered with furniture included.

Financing

Lenders review the building as well as the buyer for a condo loan, for example the HOA budget and reserves. If you plan to use a loan, ask your lender to look at this building early. I can share names of local lenders, and you choose.

Location

The towers sit at 8255 Las Vegas Blvd S, 89123, south of the Strip near the 215. The airport is a short drive north, and Henderson and the southwest valley are easy to reach on the 215.
