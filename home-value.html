How I figure out what your home is worth

  1. Pull the closest closed sales. Same neighborhood, similar size and age, sold in the last 3 to 6 months.
  2. Adjust for the differences. Pool, single-story, lot and view, upgrades, condition, HOA or guard-gate.
  3. Check the competition. What's for sale and under contract near you right now.
  4. Give you a range, with the sales behind it. You see every comparable sale I used.

Online estimates can't see inside your home, so they often miss upgrades, condition and view. If you're thinking about selling, or just want to know your equity, ask for your free valuation or check your area's monthly market report.

Why are online home value estimates different from a real valuation?

Automated estimates use public records and can't see your condition, upgrades, view, lot or HOA. A REALTOR® valuation starts from recent closed sales near you and adjusts for those differences.

What changes a Las Vegas home's value the most?

Location and recent nearby sales first, then size, single-story vs two-story, pool, lot size and view, condition and upgrades, and HOA or guard-gate. A leased solar system can also affect a sale, because the buyer has to take over the lease.

Is a home valuation the same as an appraisal?

No. An appraisal is done by a licensed appraiser, usually for a lender. A REALTOR® valuation (comparative market analysis) helps you decide a list price. Both start from recent closed sales.
