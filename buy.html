How buying with me works

  1. 1

    We talk it through

    Budget, areas, must-haves and timing. If you're financing, getting pre-approved first tells you your real price range. I can share names of local lenders, and you choose.

  2. 2

    We sign a buyer agreement

    Before we tour homes, we sign a written buyer agreement. It spells out what I do for you and how I'm paid. Read it first and ask me anything about it.

  3. 3

    You get a custom search

    I set up a search in the Las Vegas MLS for exactly what you want, so new listings and price changes that match come to your email.

  4. 4

    We tour, in person or by video

    We see the homes that fit. If you're out of town, I can walk a home on video for you.

  5. 5

    Offer and negotiation

    Before you offer, I pull the recent sales nearby so you can decide your price. I write the offer and negotiate the terms for you.

  6. 6

    Inspection, escrow and closing

    You hire a licensed home inspector, who sends you the written report. I keep track of the contract deadlines with you and title until closing. Your timeline depends on your loan and contract.

Buyer questions

Do I need a buyer agreement before touring homes?

Yes. Under the practice changes from the National Association of REALTORS® settlement, starting August 17, 2024, agents who work with buyers through the MLS need a written agreement with the buyer before touring a home. It lists the services and how the agent is paid.

How is the buyer's agent paid?

It's written in our buyer agreement before we tour. Sometimes the seller offers to pay part or all of it, and sometimes it's asked for in the offer. I'll explain your options before you sign anything.

Can I buy a home in Las Vegas without being here?

Yes. Contracts are signed electronically, I can walk a home on video for you, and closing can be done by mail-away or mobile notary. Your licensed inspector sends the written report to you.

Which school will a home be zoned for?

Schools are assigned by address through the Clark County School District. Check any address with the CCSD zoning search before you write an offer, and I'll help you look it up.

What does buying cost besides the price?

Plan for your down payment, lender and title fees, the home inspection, and HOA fees if the home has an HOA. Your lender gives you a Loan Estimate with the loan costs.
