Which part of Las Vegas fits you?

Summerlin: master-planned on the west side, near Red Rock Canyon, with trails, parks and Downtown Summerlin. Summerlin guide.

Henderson and Green Valley: the southeast side, with established neighborhoods, newer communities and guard-gated options. Henderson guide.

Southwest valley and Skye Canyon: newer construction near the mountains and the 215. Southwest market report.

Spring Valley and North Las Vegas: lower median sale prices than the valley as a whole (see the table above). Spring Valley guide.

What's different about buying in Nevada

  • Transfer tax. Clark County charges $5.10 per $1,000 of value. Your escrow officer shows the amount and who pays it.
  • HOAs are common. Many Las Vegas communities have an HOA. Read the CC&Rs for rules on rentals, RVs, parking and paint before you commit.
  • Escrow closings. Nevada closes through an escrow company, much like California.
  • Inspection time is in your contract. The due diligence period is set in your purchase agreement. Use all of it for inspections and HOA documents.

Moving checklist

  • Get a Nevada driver's license and register your vehicles within 30 days of becoming a resident (Nevada DMV).
  • Ask your tax professional how to document your change of residency from California.
  • Set up power with NV Energy and gas with Southwest Gas. Water depends on the area (Las Vegas Valley Water District, City of Henderson or City of North Las Vegas).
  • Plan for summer: electric bills are highest from June to September, so ask for the seller's past bills.

Questions California buyers ask

Is it cheaper to live in Las Vegas than in California?

For most California movers the two biggest differences are taxes and home prices. Nevada has no state personal income tax, while California taxes income at rates from 1% to 12.3%. The median single-family sale price across the Las Vegas valley was $485,000 for homes that sold from October 25 to November 24, 2026 (Las Vegas REALTORS® MLS). Compare that with what homes near you sell for, and see the cost of living guide for utilities, insurance and HOA dues.

Do I still pay California income tax after I move to Las Vegas?

Nevada has no state income tax, so once you are a Nevada resident there is no state income tax on your wages in Nevada. California can still tax income from California sources and income from the part of the year you lived there. Ask your tax professional how to document your move.

What part of Las Vegas is best for a move from California?

It depends on what you want day to day. Summerlin and Henderson are the large master-planned areas with trails, parks and newer homes. The southwest valley and Skye Canyon have newer construction near the mountains. Spring Valley and North Las Vegas have lower median prices. William Crawford, a Las Vegas REALTOR®, can walk you through each area on a video tour before you visit.

Can I buy a home in Las Vegas before I move from California?

Yes. Many out-of-state buyers tour homes by live video, sign documents electronically and close through a Nevada escrow company without being in town for every step. You can still visit before you buy if you want to see a home in person.

How soon do I need a Nevada driver's license after I move?

The Nevada DMV asks new residents to get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles within 30 days of becoming a resident.
