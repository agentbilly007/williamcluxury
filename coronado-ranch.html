About Coronado Ranch

Coronado Ranch is a master-planned community in the southwest Las Vegas valley built by American West Homes, south of the 215 near Rainbow Boulevard and Torrey Pines Drive.

It is made up of several neighborhoods, including Promontory, Kingsbridge, Tradition, Heritage and Woodbridge, with walking trails and greenbelts.

HOAs

HOA fees can be different from one street to the next, so I don't quote one number. I pull the exact fees for any home you're serious about and go through the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Schools

Coronado Ranch is in the Clark County School District, which assigns schools by street address. Check the zoning for any address with the Clark County School District before you write an offer. I'll help you look it up.
