A different kind of agent.

Backed by real service

Video gets attention, but deals get done on strategy and execution. I handle pricing, professional photography, staging guidance, negotiation, and the hundred details of a smooth close — with a 5.0★ rating from 6 Google reviews and 37 homes sold to show for it. Whether you're selling your first home or your fifth, buying across town or across the country, you get a local expert who treats your move like it's the only one that matters.

Rooted in the valley

From Summerlin to Seven Hills to Henderson, I know these neighborhoods — the streets, the HOAs, and where the real value is. As part of Huntington & Ellis, I bring the resources of an established brokerage with the attention of someone who answers his own phone.
