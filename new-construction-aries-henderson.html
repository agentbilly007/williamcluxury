What Aries is

Aries is a master-planned community of more than 1,100 acres on the east side of Henderson, Nevada, off Lake Mead Parkway near the 215 Beltway. It is being built out over years: the plan calls for 24 neighborhoods of single-family homes and townhomes, and five of them were selling when the community held its grand opening in September 2026. The first completed park, Greenbow Park, hosted that event.

PulteGroup is the lead developer. Pulte Homes is selling townhomes and single-family homes at Aries, and a Del Webb neighborhood for buyers 55 and older is listed as coming in 2027. The neighborhoods open to buyers today include Dalea, Desert Mesa, Oasis Canyon, Sora and Sundale. Pulte lists its Aries plans at roughly 1,624 to 2,519 square feet, starting in the high $300,000s.

Because it is a master plan, what you see on a visit today is a work in progress. Parks, streets and neighborhoods open in phases, so the finished community will look different from the one you tour this month. Ask the builder what is planned and what is only proposed.

Where it sits

Aries is in east Henderson, close to the Water Street District and about a ten-minute drive from MonteLago Village at Lake Las Vegas. If you are weighing a new home here against an established master plan, the Henderson guide and my master-planned communities comparison cover the alternatives.

Drive times, honestly

These are driving estimates from the Sora area without heavy traffic, so treat them as a guide and test the drive at the time you would actually make it:

  • About 10 minutes to the Water Street District in downtown Henderson
  • About 10 minutes to MonteLago Village at Lake Las Vegas
  • Roughly 30 to 35 minutes to the Las Vegas Strip
  • Roughly 30 minutes to downtown Las Vegas

It is a Henderson address, not a Strip commute. If you drive to the Strip every day, weigh that honestly.

The Modena at Sora at Aries

The Modena is the plan I toured and filmed. According to the builder's website it is a two-story home of 2,489 square feet with 4 to 5 bedrooms, 2.5 to 3.5 bathrooms and a two-car garage, and the Sora neighborhood starts in the high $400,000s. On the model I walked, there is a bedroom and bath downstairs, which matters if you want a guest room or a place for a parent, and a loft and the laundry room upstairs near the bedrooms.

What to look at in a model

A model home is dressed to sell: upgraded flooring, lighting, window coverings and furniture that are not all in the base price. When you walk the Modena, ask which finishes are standard and which are upgrades, what the lot premium is on the homesite you want, and what the closing-cost incentive requires if the builder offers one. The decisions that move your monthly payment are the lot, the options and the lender, not the sticker price on the plan.

Dalea, the townhome neighborhood, is the more compact option in Aries, and Desert Mesa, Oasis Canyon and Sundale each have their own plans and pricing. Ask the sales team for each neighborhood's current plan list.

Schools

Aries is in the Clark County School District. On the district's 2026-27 zoning map, the Sora and Dalea area is zoned to Chester T. Sewell Elementary, B. Mahlon Brown Academy of International Studies for middle school, and Basic Academy of International Studies for high school. New communities can see boundary changes as schools are built and enrollment shifts, so confirm the zoning with CCSD for the exact homesite before you write an offer.

HOA and community costs

New master plans usually have an association, and some neighborhoods add their own fees. I am not publishing a number here because dues differ by neighborhood and change as the community builds out. Ask the builder for the written HOA estimate, and the tax and special-assessment disclosures, before you commit.

Before your first visit

Bring your agent on your first visit. Most builders require your agent to register you at your first visit. It costs you nothing, and it is the one step you cannot fix after the fact.

Then, in order: get your lender pre-approval in writing first, since builder incentives often depend on using their preferred lender and you should compare that against your own; ask what is included in the base price; and read the contract's timeline language, because completion dates on a new build can move.

Pricing, plans and availability are set by the builder and subject to change; verify with the builder. Questions I get about Aries

What is Aries in Henderson?

Which schools serve Aries?

Mahlon Brown Academy of International Studies for middle school, and Basic Academy of International Studies for high school. How far is Aries from the Strip?

Plan on roughly 30 to 35 minutes to the Strip without heavy traffic, about 10 minutes to the Water Street District in downtown Henderson, and about 10 minutes to MonteLago Village at Lake Las Vegas. These are driving estimates and will vary with the time of day.

Do I need an agent to buy new construction?

No, but you should bring one. Most builders require your agent to register you at your first visit, and a buyer who walks in alone may not be able to add an agent afterward. I can walk the floor plans, the options list and the contract with you.
