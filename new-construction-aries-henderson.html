About this home

The Modena is a two-story Pulte floor plan. On the model I toured there is a bedroom and bath downstairs, an open kitchen with a large island that flows into the dining and living areas, and a loft and the laundry room upstairs with the other bedrooms.

Aries is a master-planned community of more than 1,100 acres in east Henderson, off Lake Mead Parkway near the 215 Beltway, with 24 neighborhoods planned and five selling at the September 2026 grand opening. It is about 10 minutes to the Water Street District and to MonteLago Village at Lake Las Vegas, and roughly 30 to 35 minutes to the Strip.

On the Clark County School District's 2026-27 map, the Sora area is zoned to Chester T. Sewell Elementary, B. Mahlon Brown Academy of International Studies and Basic Academy of International Studies. Confirm zoning with CCSD for your homesite.
