For a move from Hawaii, most of the work happens before you ever see a home in person. Here are the numbers that matter, and how buying from Oahu works.

Taxes

  • Income: Hawaii's top rate is 11% for 2025, on income over $325,000 for single filers and $650,000 for joint filers (Hawaii Department of Taxation). The state has announced a new 13% top bracket starting in tax year 2027 for the highest incomes. Nevada's constitution bars a state tax on the wages or personal income of individuals.
  • Capital gains: Hawaii caps the tax on capital gains at 7.25% through its alternative tax calculation. Nevada has no state income tax, so it has no state capital gains tax.
  • Estate tax: Hawaii has an estate tax with a $5,490,000 exclusion (Hawaii Department of Taxation). Nevada has none.
  • Sales tax: Hawaii charges a general excise tax instead of a sales tax: 4% state plus a 0.5% Oahu surcharge, 4.5% in all. Clark County, Nevada's sales tax is 8.375%.

Everyone's tax picture is different, so check with your tax professional before you plan around any of this.

Weather

Based on NOAA's 1991 to 2020 climate normals, Honolulu Airport averages 16.41 inches of rain a year and Las Vegas averages 4.18 inches. The bigger change is the heat: plan for hot Las Vegas summers and higher air conditioning bills in July and August.

Home prices

The Southern Nevada median single-family price was $475,000 in October 2026 (Las Vegas REALTORS, as reported by FOX5). Tell me your budget, and I'll show you what it buys here.

Getting back and forth

Southwest lists nonstop flights from Honolulu to Las Vegas at about 5 hours 54 minutes. Henderson and Seven Hills are about 20 minutes from the Las Vegas airport, which helps if you'll fly home often.

Questions people ask

How long is the flight from Honolulu to Las Vegas?

Southwest lists nonstop flights at about 5 hours 54 minutes.

Do I save on taxes moving from Hawaii to Nevada?

It depends on your situation. Nevada has no state income tax, while Hawaii's top rate was 11% for 2025. Hawaii also has an estate tax and Nevada does not. Check with your tax professional.

Can I buy a Las Vegas home without flying in?

Yes. Nevada allows electronic signatures and remote online notarization, your agent can tour homes for you on video, and a licensed home inspector you hire inspects the home.
