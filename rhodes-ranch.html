About Rhodes Ranch

Rhodes Ranch is a guard-gated master-planned community in Spring Valley, in unincorporated Clark County, about 6 miles southwest of the Strip. Its golf club opened in 1997.

Its management company described it in 2018 as guard-gated and patrolled, with a 162-acre golf club designed by Ted Robinson, a 35,000 square foot recreation center and a water park.

HOAs

HOA fees can be different from one street to the next, so I don't quote one number. I pull the exact fees for any home you're serious about and go through the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Schools

Rhodes Ranch is in the Clark County School District, which assigns schools by street address. Check the zoning for any address with the Clark County School District before you write an offer. I'll help you look it up.
