About Solera at Anthem

Solera at Anthem is a Del Webb community in the Anthem master plan in Henderson, next to Sun City Anthem. Del Webb built its 1,822 homes from 2003 to 2008, including single-family homes and attached duplex and triplex homes.

Residents use an 18,000-square-foot community center with a fitness center, indoor pool, club and hobby rooms, billiards, a computer room, and tennis and bocce courts. The City of Henderson gave the Solera at Anthem Community Association a Premier Community Award in 2016.

Age-qualified community (55+). The Las Vegas Review-Journal lists Solera at Anthem among the valley's age-qualified communities. Age rules are set by the HOA, so read the current rules in the HOA documents for any home you consider.

HOAs

HOA fees can be different from one street to the next, so I don't quote one number. I pull the exact fees for any home you're serious about and go through the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Schools

Solera at Anthem is in the Clark County School District, which assigns schools by street address. Check the zoning for any address with the Clark County School District before you write an offer. I'll help you look it up.
