What Panorama Towers is

Panorama Towers is a high-rise condo complex on Dean Martin Dr. Tower 1 is 33 stories and opened in December 2006, and Tower 2 followed in late 2007. A third tower from the same project opened in 2008 and was renamed The Martin in 2011, so it is marketed on its own today. Sources give the unit count for the two towers as about 635 to 650.

These are full-time condos, not hotel units.

What's for sale today

On November 21, 2026, 52 units were for sale at Panorama Towers (1 studio, 7 one-bedroom, 42 two-bedroom, 2 three-bedroom), from $285,000 to $13,750,000. The median asking price was $542,500. Listed floors ran from 0 to 34.

HOA fees

On the 52 active listings, the total monthly HOA (all associations combined) ran from $690 to $9,667, with a median of $1,517. Studios: median $9,667. One-bedrooms: median $762. Views and amenities

Views depend on the floor and which way the unit faces. On the 52 active listings with views entered, listing agents marked views of the city (36), the Strip (32), the mountains (23) and the pool (8). Many units list more than one. I check the direction and the floor for every unit we look at.

Building features most often listed in the MLS for Panorama Towers: guard-gated entry, pool, fitness center, spa, concierge, business center, pet park and security. Confirm what is open to owners for the unit you are buying.

Rental rules

Rental rules at Panorama Towers are set by the HOA, including any limits on short-term rentals and minimum lease terms. Local short-term rental rules also apply. Read the CC&Rs and the current rules before you buy if you might rent the unit out.

On November 21, 2026, 11 of the 52 active listings were offered with furniture included.

Financing

Lenders review the building as well as the buyer for a condo loan, for example the HOA budget and reserves. If you plan to use a loan, ask your lender to look at this building early. I can share names of local lenders, and you choose.

Location

The towers sit on Dean Martin Dr, 89103, on the west side of I-15. The Strip is across the freeway, and the Flamingo Rd and Tropicana Ave on-ramps are close by for trips to the airport, Summerlin or Henderson.
