About Red Rock Country Club

Red Rock Country Club is a guard-gated golf community in west Las Vegas (ZIP 89135). Sunrise Company built it from 1998 to 2003 on 738 acres, with about 1,100 single-family homes.

It has two 18-hole golf courses designed with Arnold Palmer. Arroyo Golf Club is open to the public, and the Mountain course is private. The developer lists a 44,000 square foot main clubhouse, a sports club with a fitness center, an aquatic center with four pools and nine lighted tennis courts.

HOAs

HOA fees can be different from one street to the next, so I don't quote one number. I pull the exact fees for any home you're serious about and go through the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Schools

Red Rock Country Club is in the Clark County School District, which assigns schools by street address. Check the zoning for any address with the Clark County School District before you write an offer. I'll help you look it up.
