About MacDonald Highlands

MacDonald Highlands is a 1,320-acre master-planned community in the foothills of Black Mountain in Henderson. It has two 24-hour guard-gated entries.

It was developed by the MacDonald family as Village III of the larger MacDonald Ranch. DragonRidge Country Club, which opened in 2000, is inside the community, along with a clubhouse, a tennis and athletic center, dog parks and parks. Ascaya and Roma Hills are next door.

HOAs

HOA fees can be different from one street to the next, so I don't quote one number. I pull the exact fees for any home you're serious about and go through the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Schools

MacDonald Highlands is in the Clark County School District, which assigns schools by street address. Check the zoning for any address with the Clark County School District before you write an offer. I'll help you look it up.
