Spring Valley is the middle of the west side

Spring Valley is not a city and not a master plan. It is a large unincorporated area of Clark County west of the Strip, roughly from Sahara Avenue south to the 215 and from Valley View west toward the Summerlin edge. Most of it was built from the 1980s through the 2000s, so it has mature streets, finished shopping, and freeway access in every direction.

What makes it worth a look is the combination: a central location, guard-gated golf communities like Rhodes Ranch and Spanish Trail, and a deep supply of homes between $400,000 and $600,000. On September 28, 2026 there were 771 single-family homes for sale across its six main zip codes, and 374 of them were between $400,000 and $600,000.

The communities, one by one

Rhodes Ranch (89148). A guard-gated golf community in southwest Spring Valley with its own course, a community center and pools. Homes from the late 1990s and 2000s, a wide range of sizes, and one of the best-known gated addresses on the west side.

Spanish Trail (89113). One of the valley's original guard-gated country club communities, built around a golf course off Tropicana and Rainbow. Larger custom and semi-custom homes and some of the highest prices in Spring Valley.

Central Spring Valley (89147). The heart of the area between Desert Inn and Tropicana, west of Rainbow: 1990s and 2000s one- and two-story homes, many gated pockets, and easy access to the 215 and Summerlin.

North and east Spring Valley (89146, 89103). The older, closer-in part of the west side, near Chinatown on Spring Mountain Road and ten minutes from the Strip. More 1970s and 1980s homes, lower HOAs, and good value for buyers who want to be central.

South of Tropicana (89118). A mix of commercial areas and pockets of larger-lot custom homes — worth knowing about if you want land without leaving the middle of the valley.

Drive times, honestly

  • Spring Valley → the Strip: about 10–20 minutes depending on the side of Spring Valley
  • Spring Valley → Harry Reid International Airport: about 15–20 minutes via the 215
  • Spring Valley → Downtown Summerlin: about 10–15 minutes
  • Spring Valley → Henderson: about 20–25 minutes on the 215

HOAs — what to expect

It varies more than anywhere else in the valley. Many older streets in 89146 and 89103 have no HOA at all; most newer tracts have a modest one; the guard-gated communities like Rhodes Ranch and Spanish Trail carry a higher fee that pays for the gate, the common areas and the amenities. I pull the exact monthly fee on any home you are serious about and read the resale package with you inside the five-day review window Nevada gives you.

Schools

Spring Valley is in the Clark County School District, which zones by street address, so neighbors on opposite sides of a major road can feed different schools. I confirm the exact zoning for any address before you write an offer.

Who Spring Valley is right for

  • Buyers who work on the Strip, at the airport or in the southwest and want a short, central commute
  • First-time and move-up buyers in the $400,000–$600,000 range
  • Buyers who want a guard gate and a golf course without Summerlin or Henderson prices
  • Investors — central west-side homes rent steadily

Questions I get about Spring Valley

How much does a home in Spring Valley cost?

On September 28, 2026 there were 771 single-family homes for sale across Spring Valley's six main zip codes (89103, 89113, 89118, 89146, 89147 and 89148). 374 were between $400,000 and $600,000, 64 were under $400,000, 209 were between $600,000 and $1 million, and 124 were over $1 million.

Is Spring Valley part of the City of Las Vegas?

No. Spring Valley is an unincorporated area of Clark County with a Las Vegas mailing address. Services like police and planning come from Clark County rather than the City of Las Vegas.

Which Spring Valley communities are guard-gated?

Rhodes Ranch and Spanish Trail are the best-known guard-gated golf communities in Spring Valley. Many smaller neighborhoods have gates without a guard.

How far is Spring Valley from the Strip?

About 10 to 20 minutes depending on which side of Spring Valley you are on — the east side near Chinatown is closest.
