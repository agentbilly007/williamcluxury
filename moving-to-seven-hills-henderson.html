If you're moving to Las Vegas and the view matters, Seven Hills is usually on the list. It's a master-planned community on the rising ground in Henderson, built around Serket Golf Club (it opened in 1997 as Rio Secco), and because the land tilts north toward the valley, many homes look straight at the Strip. For a relocation buyer, the main question isn't whether Seven Hills is nice. It's which part of Seven Hills fits your budget and your daily life.

The three kinds of Seven Hills

The entry villages

Palermo and the smaller-lot streets along the lower edge, many with single-story homes from the late 1990s. This is where Seven Hills starts.

The gated villages

Meridiana, Majestic Hills, Renaissance and their neighbors. Mostly 1990s and early 2000s homes behind gates on the middle slopes. This is the heart of the market.

The custom estates

The guard-gated enclaves on the upper slopes and along the golf course, including Marquis Seven Hills. Large custom and semi-custom homes, most well over $1 million.

Compare the villages side by side, with today's price ranges, in Seven Hills villages compared, or see more home tours on my Seven Hills neighborhood page.

Daily life and drive times

  • Harry Reid International Airport: about 20 minutes via the 215 and I-15
  • The Strip: about 25 minutes outside of rush hour
  • The District at Green Valley Ranch: 5 to 10 minutes
  • St. Rose Parkway shopping and hospitals: about 5 minutes

That airport time is a big reason Seven Hills works for people who still fly back for work or family.

HOAs: the part relocation buyers ask about most

Every home is in the Seven Hills master association, and the gated and guard-gated villages add their own HOA on top. Fees vary a lot between an open street and a guard-gated enclave, so I don't quote one number. For any home you're serious about, I pull the exact fees and read the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109), so we use that window carefully, especially when you're buying from out of state.

Schools

Seven Hills is in the Clark County School District, which assigns schools by street address. Many Seven Hills homes feed Coronado High School, but it depends on the street, so check the zoning for any address with the Clark County School District before you write an offer. I'll help you look it up.

Buying in Seven Hills from out of state

Many relocation buyers shortlist villages over video before they fly in. I send drive-through footage at different times of day, check the actual sight line for the view on each home, and can be there to give your licensed home inspector access. Nevada allows electronic signatures and remote online notarization, so much of the process can happen from wherever you live now. The full step-by-step is in my guide to buying a Las Vegas home without seeing it in person.

Questions people ask

Is Seven Hills in Las Vegas or Henderson?

Seven Hills is in the City of Henderson, in the southeast part of the Las Vegas valley, about 20 minutes from Harry Reid International Airport.

How much do homes in Seven Hills cost?

On November 9, 2026, single-family homes for sale in Seven Hills ranged from $438,000 to over $7 million, with a middle list price of $849,900. Prices change weekly, so ask me for today's numbers.

Is Seven Hills guard-gated?

Parts of it. Seven Hills has open streets, gated villages and guard-gated estate enclaves, each with different HOA fees.

Do all Seven Hills homes have Strip views?

No. It depends on the street and the lot. I check the actual view on each home, not just the listing photos.
