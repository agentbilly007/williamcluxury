Living in Henderson, NV
A 2026 Local's Guide

By William Crawford June 20, 2026 7 min read

Henderson isn't just a suburb of Las Vegas — it's a city of its own, and for a lot of my clients it's the answer to "I want the Vegas lifestyle without living on top of the Strip." Master-planned, clean, safe, and packed with parks and trails, Henderson keeps landing on national "best places to live" lists for good reason. Here's the honest, on-the-ground guide to living here in 2026.

Why People Move to Henderson

Henderson sits just southeast of Las Vegas, and the contrast is immediate. Where parts of the valley grew up fast and unplanned, Henderson was largely built as connected master-planned communities — Green Valley, Anthem, Seven Hills, Inspirada, Cadence — each with its own parks, retail, and schools. The result is a city that feels organized, walkable in pockets, and family-friendly without being boring.

You still get everything that makes Nevada attractive: no state income tax, relatively low property taxes, 300+ days of sunshine, and a 20–25 minute drive to the Strip or the airport when you want it. You just come home to quiet streets, mountain views, and one of the lowest crime rates of any large city in the Southwest.

The Best Neighborhoods in Henderson

Henderson is big, and "Henderson" can mean very different price points and lifestyles depending on where you land. Here's how I break it down for clients:

Green Valley & Green Valley Ranch

The original master plan and still one of the most popular. Mature trees, established schools, and The District at Green Valley Ranch for walkable dining and shopping. Great for families and first-time buyers who want a turnkey, convenient location. Expect mostly resale homes here rather than new construction.

Seven Hills

Guard-gated luxury on the elevated southern edge of Henderson, with golf, big lots, and some of the best valley-and-Strip views in the city. This is where you'll find a lot of move-up and luxury buyers. (I wrote a separate guide to the best restaurants near Seven Hills if you want a taste of the area.)

Anthem & Sun City Anthem

Anthem offers hillside homes and country-club living, while Sun City Anthem is one of the premier 55+ active-adult communities in Nevada — resort amenities, golf, and a strong social calendar. If you're retiring to Henderson, start here.

MacDonald Highlands

The address for ultra-luxury. Guard-gated, custom estates, the DragonRidge Country Club, and dramatic elevated views. This is the high end of the Henderson market and where a lot of the valley's trophy homes trade.

Inspirada & Cadence

The two communities driving Henderson's newer growth. Both are packed with parks, trails, and community amenities, and both still have active new-construction phases. Great value for families who want a newer home and a true neighborhood feel.

Lake Las Vegas

A resort-style enclave built around a 320-acre lake — Mediterranean architecture, waterfront dining, two hotels, and golf. It lives like a vacation. Popular with second-home buyers and anyone who wants something that doesn't feel like the rest of the valley.

Schools in Henderson

Schools are the number-one question I get from relocating families, and they're a real draw here. Henderson is served by the Clark County School District but is home to several of the highest-rated public schools in the valley, plus strong charter and private options. Communities like Green Valley, Seven Hills, and Anthem are especially popular with families specifically for their school zoning. If schools are your priority, tell me which grade levels matter most and I'll help you target the right zones before you fall in love with a house in the wrong one.

Cost of Living

Henderson runs a little above the Las Vegas valley average on home prices — you're paying for the master-planned quality of life — but it's still a bargain compared to coastal California. A household relocating from Los Angeles, the Bay Area, or Seattle almost always finds they get dramatically more home for the money, and the no-income-tax math compounds every year. Utilities, groceries, and gas are all roughly in line with national averages.

Lifestyle: Parks, Trails & Dining

This is where Henderson quietly wins. The city maintains an extensive network of parks and the paved River Mountains Loop and pedestrian trail system, so morning walks and bike rides are part of the routine. Downtown's Water Street District has been revitalized with restaurants and events, The District at GVR covers upscale shopping and dining, and you're minutes from hiking at the McCullough Hills and Sloan Canyon. It's an outdoorsy, active, family-forward way to live — with world-class entertainment 20 minutes up the 215 whenever you want it.

The Henderson Housing Market in 2026

Henderson remains one of the most resilient submarkets in the valley. Inventory is still on the tighter side, well-priced homes in the popular communities move quickly, and the luxury tier ($1M+) offers more negotiating room than it did a couple of years ago. New construction in Cadence and Inspirada gives buyers a new-home option with builder incentives, while resale in Green Valley and Seven Hills holds value thanks to location and schools. For a current snapshot of the wider valley, see my Las Vegas market update.

Is Henderson Right for You?

Move here if you want safe, master-planned living, top schools, parks and trails out your front door, and an easy drive to the Strip and airport. Look elsewhere if you want to be in the middle of nightlife and the high-rise scene — that's more of a Las Vegas proper play, and I'm happy to point you there too.
