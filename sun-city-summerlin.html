About Sun City Summerlin

Sun City Summerlin is a Del Webb community inside the Summerlin master plan on the west side of Las Vegas. Del Webb built its 7,779 homes on 2,400 acres from 1989 to 1999, near the Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area.

The community association lists three 18-hole golf courses, four fitness centers, five indoor and outdoor pools, and four clubhouses with tennis courts, a ballroom, a softball field and a 312-seat theater.

Age-qualified community (55+). The Sun City Summerlin Community Association's age policy requires at least one resident age 55 or older in each home, and no one under 19 may live there permanently. Age rules are set by the HOA, so read the current rules in the HOA documents for any home you consider.

HOAs

HOA fees can be different from one street to the next, so I don't quote one number. I pull the exact fees for any home you're serious about and go through the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Schools

Sun City Summerlin is in the Clark County School District, which assigns schools by street address. Check the zoning for any address with the Clark County School District before you write an offer. I'll help you look it up.
