What the numbers say

  • Size: the median home for sale ran from 1,248 square feet in Solera at Anthem to 2,100 in Siena. The median home had two bedrooms in most of the six, two or three in Solera and three in Sun City Aliante.
  • Price: median asking prices ran from $379,900 in Sun City Aliante to $775,000 in Siena.
  • Age of the homes: Sun City Summerlin is the oldest, with most homes built from 1989 to 1998. Solera and Sun City Aliante are the newest of the six, mostly built from 2004 to 2007.

Prices change every week, so ask me for today's numbers on any community.

Age rules and HOAs

Each community's HOA sets its age and occupancy rules, so read the current rules in the HOA documents for any home you consider. Federal rules let a community call itself 55+ when at least 80% of occupied homes have at least one resident 55 or older (24 CFR 100.305). I pull the current HOA fees and the resale package for any home you're serious about. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).
