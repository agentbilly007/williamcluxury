For comfortable showings, the mild months in Las Vegas are October through April, when the normal daily high at the airport stays at or below 81.2°F (NOAA 1991-2020 normals). From June through August the normal high is 99.4°F to 104.5°F, so tour in the morning. Touring in summer

  • Go early. Mornings are cooler than afternoons, though the normal July low is still 82.0°F. Book the first showings of the day.
  • Plan fewer homes per trip. Short drives between homes, water in the car, and a break in the middle.
  • Never leave a person or a pet in a parked car. The National Weather Service warns that a closed car heats up fast, even with the windows cracked.
  • Can't come in summer? I tour homes for you live on FaceTime or send a full walkthrough. See video home tours.

What a summer showing tells you

  • The AC. You feel how the home holds the temperature on a hot day, upstairs and down. A licensed home inspector you hire checks the system and writes the report.
  • The backyard sun. You see where the afternoon sun lands, which matters for a patio, a pool and the rooms facing it.
  • The pool. You see the pool in use season, with the equipment running.
  • The power bills. Ask for the past 12 months of bills. Summer months are usually the highest. See cost of living in Las Vegas.

Touring in the mild months

October through April, you can tour any time of day, walk the neighborhood and visit parks and trails in comfort. Winter nights get cool: the normal December low is 39.6°F. If you're moving from somewhere cold, see the move guides on relocating to Las Vegas.

Questions people ask

What is the best time of year to tour homes in Las Vegas?

For comfortable weather, October through April, when the normal daily high at Harry Reid International Airport stays at or below 81.2°F (NOAA 1991-2020 normals). Homes are bought and sold all year.

How hot does it get in Las Vegas in summer?

The normal high is 99.4°F in June, 104.5°F in July and 102.8°F in August at the airport, with a normal July low of 82.0°F (NOAA 1991-2020 normals).

What time of day should I tour homes in summer?

In the morning. Book the first showings of the day, keep water in the car, and never leave a person or a pet in a parked car.
