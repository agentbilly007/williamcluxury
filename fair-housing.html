Equal Housing Opportunity

William Crawford and Huntington & Ellis, A Real Estate Agency are committed to the letter and spirit of U.S. policy for the achievement of equal housing opportunity. We support and comply with the Fair Housing Act, which prohibits discrimination in the sale, rental, and financing of housing based on race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, or national origin — as well as Nevada state protections.

Every client is welcomed and served with the same professionalism and care, without exception.
