Las Vegas Master-Planned
Communities Compared (2026)

By William Crawford April 14, 2026 6 min read

Las Vegas has a reputation built on the Strip — but most of the valley lives in master-planned suburbs 15 to 30 minutes away. Here is how five of them compare on location, housing, HOAs, amenities, and price.

What to Compare

Before comparing communities, here are the facts worth lining up side by side:

  • Location and commute: Drive times to the Strip, the airport, and major employers vary a lot across the valley.
  • Home styles and age: Older master plans have mature landscaping and larger lots; newer ones have newer construction and builder phases still selling.
  • HOAs: Most master-planned communities have HOAs that maintain common areas, parks, and pools. Fees and rules vary by village — review the resale package.
  • Parks and trails: Las Vegas has strong outdoor infrastructure in its master-planned communities.
  • School zoning: Clark County School District assigns schools by street address, and boundaries change. Confirm the zoned schools for any specific address with CCSD.

Summerlin

Best for: Buyers who want a large master plan with trails, community events, and quick access to Red Rock Canyon.

Summerlin is the valley's flagship master plan on the western edge, up against Red Rock Canyon. Over 230 parks, 150+ miles of trails, and Downtown Summerlin for shopping and dining. The community is massive (22,500 acres), so there are options at every price point.

Median Price~$650,000
LocationWest valley
StyleMaster-planned suburban

Henderson (Anthem / Seven Hills)

Best for: Buyers who want an established master plan in the southeast valley, with guard-gated options and elevated views.

Anthem and Seven Hills sit in the hills of Henderson, with guard-gated sections, community centers, and pools. The Anthem community center rivals many private club facilities. Close to Lake Mead and the McCullough Hills for outdoor recreation.

Median Price~$600,000
LocationSoutheast valley
StyleMaster-planned, gated options

Green Valley (Henderson)

Best for: Buyers who want mature trees, established streets, and walkable dining and shopping.

Green Valley was one of the valley's first master-planned communities, and it shows — mature landscaping and neighborhoods that newer developments are still growing into. The District at Green Valley Ranch is a walkable outdoor center with restaurants and shops, and the airport is about 15 minutes away.

Median Price~$480,000
LocationHenderson, near the 215
StyleMature, walkable suburban

Skye Canyon (Northwest Las Vegas)

Best for: Buyers who want newer construction and mountain views at a lower price point than Summerlin.

Skye Canyon is one of the valley's newer master-planned communities. The community park has pools, a splash pad, a fitness center, and year-round events. Newer homes and mountain views, at a lower median price than Summerlin.

Median Price~$550,000
LocationNorthwest valley
StyleNewer construction

Spring Valley (Peccole Ranch / Canyon Gate)

Best for: Buyers who want a central location and more home per dollar.

Spring Valley offers more home per dollar than Summerlin or Henderson, with gated community options starting under $500K, including Canyon Gate and Peccole Ranch. Central to major employers, hospitals, the 215, and the Strip.

Median Price~$430,000
LocationCentral west valley
StyleSuburban, gated options

How to Choose

If you want the largest master plan and Red Rock access: Summerlin. If you want the southeast valley with gated options: Anthem or Seven Hills. If you want to stretch your dollar: Green Valley or Spring Valley. If you want new construction: Skye Canyon.
