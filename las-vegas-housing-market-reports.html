Questions people ask

How many homes sold in Las Vegas last month?

From October 25, 2026 to November 24, 2026, 1,294 single-family homes sold in the Las Vegas valley at a median price of $485,000, based on the Las Vegas REALTORS® MLS.

How often are these reports updated?

Once a month, from the Las Vegas REALTORS® MLS. Each area page shows the date the numbers were pulled and keeps past months in an archive table.

What areas are covered?

Las Vegas (the whole valley), Henderson, Summerlin, North Las Vegas, Southwest Las Vegas, Northwest Las Vegas and Spring Valley. Each report lists the ZIP codes or MLS communities it counts.

What does months of inventory mean?

Homes for sale divided by homes sold in the last 30 days. It shows how long today's listings would last at the recent pace of sales if no new homes were listed.
