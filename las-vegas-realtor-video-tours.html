Two kinds of video tour

  • Live tour for you. You pick the home. I walk it with you on FaceTime, and you tell me where to point the camera. Open the closets, run the water, step into the backyard, look at the house next door. Ask anything while we're there.
  • Recorded walkthrough. If the time difference doesn't work, I film a steady walkthrough and send it to you. You can share it with your family and watch it as many times as you like.

Both are part of working with me as your buyer's agent. My public tours on the video tours page show how I film.

What I show you on a tour

  • The front of the home, the street and the homes on each side
  • Every room, with the windows and the light at that time of day
  • The backyard, the pool equipment, the side yards and the view over the wall
  • The garage, the water heater and the AC units, so you see their labels and general condition
  • The drive in: the gate, the nearest shopping and the main road, if you want it

What video can't tell you

A camera can't measure noise from a nearby road, a smell, or how a room feels. I tell you what I notice in person. A licensed home inspector you hire does the inspection and writes the report. I can give the inspector access and, with their OK, send you video. If something matters a lot to you, a visit before closing is always an option.

Buying from out of state

Nevada allows electronic signatures (NRS 719.240) and remote online notarization (NRS 240.181 to 240.206). Your escrow officer coordinates the signing with you. Your timeline depends on your loan and your contract. The full process is on buying a Las Vegas home sight unseen.
