Buyers moving from Los Angeles often mention the same three reasons: home prices, taxes, and wanting more space without giving up sunshine. The drive up I-15 is short enough that family and work trips back to LA are easy. Here's what the move really looks like on paper.

Home prices

The median sold price for an existing single-family home in Los Angeles County was $994,670 in September 2026 (California Association of REALTORS). In Southern Nevada, the median single-family price was $475,000 in October 2026 (Las Vegas REALTORS, as reported by FOX5). Medians hide a lot of variety on both sides, so the better question is what your budget buys in the neighborhoods you'd actually live in. I'm happy to pull that for you.

Income tax

Nevada does not tax personal income. California's top income tax rate is 12.3%, plus an extra 1% on income over $1 million (tax year 2025, California Franchise Tax Board). How much you'd save depends on your income and situation, so talk with your tax professional before you plan around it.

Sales tax

The sales tax rate in Clark County, Nevada is 8.375%. Los Angeles County's rate rose to 10.25% on October 1, 2026, and many LA County cities are higher (California Department of Tax and Fee Administration).

Property tax: different rules

In California, Prop 13 limits how much your assessed value can grow each year, and a purchase resets it. Nevada works differently: on the home you live in as your primary residence, the yearly increase in your property tax bill is capped at 3% (other property can have a higher cap). Clark County explains the abatement on its website. When we look at a home, I show you the current tax bill so you're not guessing.

Where LA buyers tend to land

  • Summerlin, for trails, Red Rock Canyon and a master-planned feel
  • Henderson, including Seven Hills, Green Valley and Anthem
  • Southern Highlands and the southwest, for quick access to I-15 toward LA
  • New construction, for buyers who want a brand-new home

Your first month in Nevada

New residents have 30 days to get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles (Nevada DMV). Make a DMV appointment early. Set up NV Energy and your water service before move-in, and expect your biggest utility bills in July and August when the air conditioning runs.

Buying before you move

Many LA buyers start the search from home. Nevada allows electronic signatures and remote online notarization, and I can tour homes for you on video and be there to give your licensed home inspector access. See how buying a Las Vegas home from out of state works.

Questions people ask

Is Las Vegas cheaper than Los Angeles?

On home prices, generally yes. The LA County single-family median was $994,670 in September 2026, and the Southern Nevada median was $475,000 in October 2026. Costs vary by neighborhood, so compare the specific areas you'd live in.

Does Nevada have a state income tax?

No. Nevada does not tax personal income. Check with your tax professional about your own situation.

How long do I have to switch my license and registration?

New Nevada residents have 30 days to get a Nevada driver's license and register their vehicles, according to the Nevada DMV.

Can I buy a Las Vegas home while still living in LA?

Yes. Nevada allows electronic signatures and remote online notarization, and your agent can tour homes for you on video, and a licensed home inspector you hire inspects the home.
