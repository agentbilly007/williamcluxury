About Siena

Siena is a 667-acre community in Summerlin South with 2,001 single-family homes and villas. It runs from Flamingo Road to south of Tropicana Avenue, between Town Center Drive and Hualapai Way.

Siena Golf Course, a public 18-hole course, runs through the community. The community center has a library, a ballroom, and art, ceramics, sewing, music, billiards and game rooms, next to a 16,000-square-foot fitness center.

Age-qualified community (55+). The Siena Community Association describes Siena as a 55+ community. Age rules are set by the HOA, so read the current rules in the HOA documents for any home you consider.

HOAs

HOA fees can be different from one street to the next, so I don't quote one number. I pull the exact fees for any home you're serious about and go through the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Schools

Siena is in the Clark County School District, which assigns schools by street address. Check the zoning for any address with the Clark County School District before you write an offer. I'll help you look it up.
