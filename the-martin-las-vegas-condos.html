What The Martin is

The Martin opened in 2008 as the third tower of the Panorama Towers project, then called Panorama Tower North. It was renamed The Martin in January 2011. It is 45 stories with 372 condos. These are full-time condos, not hotel units. For the two original towers next door, see Panorama Towers.

HOA fees

On the 12 active listings, the total monthly HOA (all associations combined) ran from $850 to $1,649, with a median of $942. Listings most often show the fee covering concierge and grounds upkeep. Ask for the current budget, the reserve study and what's included for the exact unit.

Views and amenities

Views depend on the floor and which way the unit faces. On the 12 active listings with views entered, listing agents marked views of the city (10), the Strip (10) and the mountains (8). Many units list more than one. I check the direction and the floor for every unit we look at.

Building features most often listed in the MLS for The Martin: fitness center, pool, security, exercise room, business center, spa, indoor spa and concierge. Confirm what is open to owners for the unit you are buying.

Rental rules

Rental rules at The Martin are set by the HOA, including any limits on short-term rentals and minimum lease terms. Local short-term rental rules also apply. Read the CC&Rs and the current rules before you buy if you might rent the unit out.

On November 21, 2026, 1 of the 12 active listings were offered with furniture included.

Financing

Lenders review the building as well as the buyer for a condo loan, for example the HOA budget and reserves. If you plan to use a loan, ask your lender to look at this building early. I can share names of local lenders, and you choose.

Location

The tower sits at 4471 Dean Martin Dr, 89103, on the west side of I-15 across from the Strip, next to Panorama Towers. The Flamingo Rd on-ramp is close by.
