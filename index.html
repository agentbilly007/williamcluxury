Common Questions

The things people ask first

Am I locked in if I reach out?

No. The first conversation and the valuation are free, with no obligation. Plenty of people talk to me a year or more before they actually sell. Nothing is signed until you've seen the pricing and the plan and you're ready.

What is it actually like to work with you?

Quiet, mostly. I price your home from real neighborhood numbers, put the marketing plan in writing before we list, send you a clear update every week, and pick up when you call. The goal is that you never have to chase me for an answer.

Why does video marketing help sell a home faster?

A cinematic listing video shows a home the way buyers experience it in person, and it can be distributed on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube to reach tens of thousands of buyers beyond the MLS. My listing videos regularly reach 20,000 to 60,000 views each — demand that photos alone can't create.

What areas do you serve?

I list and sell homes throughout the Las Vegas Valley, including Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, Green Valley, Spring Valley, and North Las Vegas.
