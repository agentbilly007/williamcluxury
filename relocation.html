Questions

What relocation buyers ask me.

Can I buy a home in Las Vegas without flying in first?

Yes. Contracts in Nevada are signed electronically, and closing can be handled by mail-away or a mobile notary wherever you are. I can attend the inspection on your behalf and send you a video walkthrough. Many buyers skip the scouting trip and buy remotely.

How long does a relocation to Las Vegas take?

Most relocations run on roughly a 60 to 90 day runway from deciding you are serious to getting the keys.

Does Nevada have a state income tax?

No. Nevada has no state income tax on wages, retirement income or capital gains. Everyone's tax situation is different, so check with your tax professional.

Which Las Vegas area is right for me?

It depends on your lifestyle, commute, schools and budget. Summerlin, Henderson, Southwest and Mountain's Edge, and the Strip-corridor high-rises each suit different buyers. I walk relocation clients through the options over video so you can shortlist before you book a flight.

Should I get pre-approved before I start looking?

Yes. Get pre-approved with a lender licensed in Nevada before touring homes. Cash buyers line up proof of funds instead. I can connect you with lenders who work with relocation buyers.

What costs should I plan for besides the purchase price?

Plan for long-distance movers (book 4 to 6 weeks out), vehicle registration and a Nevada driver's license, utility setup, and higher cooling costs in July and August.
