About Summerlin West

Summerlin West is the newest part of Summerlin, on higher ground just west of the 215. Its neighborhoods include Redpoint (sales began 2020) and Kestrel (2022), which sits above 3,000 feet, plus the newer Grand Park area (2024).

Grand Park is the largest park in Summerlin, and part of it opened in 2026. Other new parks include Kestrel Creek Arroyo, The Hub and Bluebird Park. Heritage at Stonebridge is a 55+ neighborhood here, so check the age rules for any home you look at. The Spring Mountains and Red Rock Canyon are to the west.

HOAs

HOA fees can be different from one street to the next, so I don't quote one number. I pull the exact fees for any home you're serious about and go through the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Schools

Summerlin West is in the Clark County School District, which assigns schools by street address. Check the zoning for any address with the Clark County School District before you write an offer. I'll help you look it up.
