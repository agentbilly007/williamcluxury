Moving from Chicago to Las Vegas trades snow shovels for air conditioning, and a state income tax for none. Here's how the two compare.

Weather

Based on NOAA's 1991 to 2020 climate normals, O'Hare averages 38.4 inches of snow and 37.86 inches of rain and melted snow a year. Las Vegas averages 0.2 inches of snow and 4.18 inches of rain. Plan for hot summers and higher air conditioning bills in July and August.

Taxes

  • Income: Illinois has a flat 4.95% income tax (Illinois Department of Revenue). Nevada's constitution bars a state tax on the wages or personal income of individuals.
  • Estate tax: Illinois has an estate tax that applies to estates over $4 million (Illinois Attorney General). Nevada has none.
  • Sales tax: Chicago's combined rate is 10.50% since August 1, 2026: 6.25% state, 1.75% Cook County, 1.25% City of Chicago and 1.25% regional transit. Clark County, Nevada is 8.375%.

Everyone's tax picture is different, so check with your tax professional before you plan around any of this.

Home prices

The Southern Nevada median single-family price was $475,000 in October 2026 (Las Vegas REALTORS, as reported by FOX5). Tell me your budget, and I'll show you what it buys here.

Getting back and forth

Southwest lists nonstop flights from Chicago Midway to Las Vegas at about 3 hours 53 minutes. Henderson and Seven Hills are about 20 minutes from the Las Vegas airport.

Questions people ask

Does it snow in Las Vegas?

Rarely. NOAA's 1991 to 2020 normals show about 0.2 inches a year at the Las Vegas airport, compared with 38.4 inches at O'Hare.

Do I save on taxes moving from Chicago to Las Vegas?

It depends on your situation. Nevada has no state income tax, and Illinois has a flat 4.95% income tax. Sales tax is 8.375% in Clark County and 10.50% in Chicago. Check with your tax professional.

How long is the flight?

Southwest lists nonstop flights from Chicago Midway to Las Vegas at about 3 hours 53 minutes.

Can I buy without flying in?

Yes. Nevada allows electronic signatures and remote online notarization, your agent can tour homes for you on video, and a licensed home inspector you hire inspects the home.
