Questions people ask

How many homes are for sale in Las Vegas and Henderson right now?

On November 10, 2026, 5,326 single-family homes were for sale across Henderson, Summerlin, northwest, southwest and North Las Vegas, based on the Las Vegas REALTORS® MLS.

What is the median sale price in Henderson?

From May 14, 2026 to November 10, 2026, the median single-family sale price in Henderson-area ZIP codes was $535,000.

How long do homes take to sell in Summerlin?

Single-family homes that sold in Summerlin from May 14, 2026 to November 10, 2026 had a median of 29 days on the market.
