When out-of-state buyers say "Seven Hills," they usually picture the view homes on the hill. But the community runs from gated single-story homes in the $400s to custom estates past $7 million. The village decides your price, your HOA and your neighbors, so it's worth knowing the differences before you tour.

How to think about the tiers

Palermo and the lower villages

Gated homes from the late 1990s. Four of the five for sale today are single-story, about 1,200 to 1,450 square feet. This is where Seven Hills starts, with a gate and a short drive to the airport at a lower price than the rest of the community.

Majestic Hills, Meridiana and Renaissance

The middle of the market. Larger homes from about 1997 to 2003, behind gates, with more of the Strip and valley views Seven Hills is known for. Meridiana stands out for single-story living: four of the five Meridiana homes for sale today are single-story, from about 2,100 to 3,250 square feet. Renaissance tops this tier, with homes from about $950,000 to $1.5 million today.

The Estates, Terracina and La Vita

The custom-home enclaves. Every listing in these three villages today is over $2.3 million, most on big lots with large custom homes. The Marquis home for sale today lists La Vita as its village association, so the table groups them together.

HOA dues: two layers

Every home pays the Seven Hills master association (most of today's listings show $73 a month), and most also pay a village association on top. In today's listings, village dues ran from under $50 a month in the lower villages to about $500 a month in The Estates. Agents type these numbers into the MLS by hand, so confirm the real amounts in the HOA resale package. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive it (NRS 116.4109).

Schools

Every one of the 55 listings today names Coronado High School. School zones are set by the Clark County School District by street address, so check the zoning for any home with the district before you write an offer. I'll help you look it up.

Questions people ask

What is the cheapest village in Seven Hills?

On November 9, 2026, the lowest-priced homes for sale were in Palermo, from $438,000. Prices change weekly.

Which Seven Hills villages are custom estates?

The Estates at Seven Hills, Terracina and La Vita (including Marquis). Every listing in those villages on November 9, 2026 was over $2.3 million.

How much are Seven Hills HOA dues?

Every home pays the master association, and most also pay a village HOA. In current listings, village dues ran from under $50 to about $500 a month. Confirm the exact amounts in the HOA resale package.

Which high school serves Seven Hills?

All 55 listings on November 9, 2026 named Coronado High School, but zones are set by street address, so confirm for each home.
