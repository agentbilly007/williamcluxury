What Palms Place is

Palms Place opened in 2008 as the third tower on the Palms property. It is 47 stories with 599 condo-hotel units, including 21 penthouses on the top floors. A moving walkway connects it to the Palms casino resort.

What's for sale today

On November 21, 2026, 63 units were for sale at Palms Place (41 studios, 16 one-bedroom, 4 two-bedroom, 2 three-bedroom), from $168,888 to $2,950,000. The median asking price was $238,000. Listed floors ran from 3 to 58.

Views and amenities

Views depend on the floor and which way the unit faces. On the 63 active listings with views entered, listing agents marked views of the city (47), the Strip (44), the mountains (34), a park or greenbelt (1) and the pool (1). Many units list more than one. I check the direction and the floor for every unit we look at.

Building features most often listed in the MLS for Palms Place: pool, fitness center, concierge, pool cabanas, guest parking, security, spa and business center. Confirm what is open to owners for the unit you are buying.

Using the unit and renting it out

Owners at Palms Place can usually stay in their unit, and many place it in the hotel's rental program when they're not using it. On November 21, 2026, 42 of the 63 active listings mentioned a rental program in the listing remarks, and 57 were listed with furniture included. How the program works, its fees and how revenue is shared are set by the hotel program and the HOA, and they can change. Rental income is never certain. Read the rental program agreement and the CC&Rs before you write an offer.

Financing a condo-hotel

Many lenders treat condo-hotels differently than regular condos, so cash or specialty loans are common. If you plan to use a loan, talk to a lender about this building before you pick a unit, and budget extra time.

Location

Palms Place sits at 4381 W Flamingo Rd, 89103, west of I-15 and next to the Palms. The Strip is about a mile east, and the I-15 on-ramp at Flamingo is close by.
