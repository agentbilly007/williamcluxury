Living in Seven Hills

Perched on the elevated southern edge of Henderson, Seven Hills is a master-planned community known for its guard-gated enclaves, custom estates, and some of the best views in the valley — city lights, the Strip, and the surrounding mountains. It's a place that feels removed and private, yet sits minutes from shopping, dining, and the freeway.

The community wraps around the Rio Secco Golf Club, a Rees Jones championship course, and offers a mix of gated subdivisions, semi-custom homes, and luxury estates. Buyers are drawn to Seven Hills for the combination of prestige, privacy, and location — a rare thing even in Henderson.

What you'll find here

Home styles range from low-maintenance gated townhomes and single-story homes to sprawling custom estates on view lots. It's a favorite for professionals, families, and second-home buyers who want luxury without leaving the conveniences of Henderson behind.
