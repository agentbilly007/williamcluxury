Many Bay Area buyers come to Las Vegas with equity, a remote or hybrid job, and a long list of things they're tired of paying for. The move is a big change in climate and pace, so it's worth looking at the real numbers before you decide.

Income tax

Nevada does not tax personal income. California's top income tax rate is 12.3%, plus an extra 1% on income over $1 million (tax year 2025, California Franchise Tax Board). Your savings depend on your situation, so check with your tax professional.

Sales tax

Clark County's sales tax rate is 8.375%. In the Bay Area, San Francisco is 8.625% and San Jose is 10% (California Department of Tax and Fee Administration, rates in effect October 2026).

What surprises Bay Area buyers

  • The space. A Bay Area budget can go further here, sometimes into homes with a yard, a pool or a view here.
  • The heat. Summers are hot and dry. Plan for higher cooling bills in July and August.
  • HOAs. Many Las Vegas neighborhoods have an HOA. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive the HOA resale package (NRS 116.4109), and I read it with you.

Where Bay Area buyers tend to look

  • Summerlin, for trails and Red Rock Canyon
  • Seven Hills and Henderson, for views and quick airport access
  • Lake Las Vegas, for a resort feel on the water
  • Strip-area high-rises, for lock-and-leave living if you'll split time

Questions people ask

How much cheaper are homes in Las Vegas than the Bay Area?

In September 2026 the Bay Area region single-family median was $1,325,000 (C.A.R.), and the Southern Nevada median was $475,000 in October 2026. The difference depends on the exact areas you compare.

Does Nevada have a state income tax?

No. Nevada does not tax personal income. Check with your tax professional about your own situation.

Can I keep my Bay Area job and live in Las Vegas?

Many remote and hybrid workers do. Check your employer's rules on working from another state.

Do I need to fly in to buy?

Not necessarily. Nevada allows electronic signatures and remote online notarization, and your agent can tour homes for you on video, and a licensed home inspector you hire inspects the home.
