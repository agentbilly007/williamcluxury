About Ascaya

Ascaya is a private, guard-gated community of custom homes in Henderson, on 664 acres in the McCullough Range at elevations up to 3,200 feet. Its sales center opened in 2014, and it borders Sloan Canyon National Conservation Area.

Ascaya lists a 23,000 square foot clubhouse with private dining and fitness, a zero-edge pool with lap lanes, tennis and pickleball courts, a family park, and miles of maintained trails.

HOAs

HOA fees can be different from one street to the next, so I don't quote one number. I pull the exact fees for any home you're serious about and go through the HOA resale package with you. In Nevada you can cancel within five days after you receive that package (NRS 116.4109).

Schools

Ascaya is in the Clark County School District, which assigns schools by street address. Check the zoning for any address with the Clark County School District before you write an offer. I'll help you look it up.
