Pick Summerlin if you want one planned community on the west side near Red Rock Canyon, with a single master association. Pick Henderson if you want a wider range of prices and neighborhoods, from older Green Valley streets to new master plans, closer to the airport and Lake Mead. William Crawford, a Las Vegas REALTOR® who works in both, can tour homes in each for you on video so you can compare.

The short version

  • What it is. Summerlin is a 22,500-acre master plan started in 1990 by the company now called Howard Hughes. Most of it is in the City of Las Vegas, and Summerlin South is in unincorporated Clark County. Henderson is its own city, incorporated in 1953, with dozens of neighborhoods and several master plans (Green Valley, Anthem, Seven Hills, Inspirada, Cadence and more).
  • Price. From May 14 through November 10, 2026, the median single-family sale price was $784,670 in Summerlin and $535,000 in Henderson-area ZIP codes. Henderson had 335 homes for sale under $450,000 on November 10; Summerlin had 13.
  • Location. Summerlin is on the west edge of the valley, next to Red Rock Canyon. Henderson is southeast, about 15 minutes from Harry Reid International Airport from most of Green Valley, and 20 to 30 minutes from Lake Mead.
  • HOAs. Every Summerlin home pays into a Summerlin community association, and many villages add their own fee. Henderson has HOA neighborhoods and older streets with no HOA. See no-HOA homes in Henderson.

Property tax rates

Clark County sets a rate for each tax district. For 2026-27: City of Henderson 2.9613 per $100 of assessed value, Summerlin Town 2.9328, and City of Las Vegas 3.2782. Parts of Summerlin are in each Summerlin district, so check the district on any home. Your bill also depends on the 3% cap for a primary residence. Details are on Las Vegas property tax. Taxes depend on your situation, so check with your tax professional.

The feel of each

Summerlin is organized by villages, each with parks, trails and community centers. The newest building is in Summerlin West, on higher ground west of the 215. Downtown Summerlin has shopping and dining, and Red Rock Canyon is close to the west side. Read Summerlin, Summerlin North, Summerlin South and Summerlin West.

Henderson is a mix. Green Valley is older and central, Anthem and Seven Hills sit on the slopes in the south, Inspirada and Cadence are newer master plans, and Lake Las Vegas has homes on the water. Read Henderson, Green Valley, Seven Hills, Anthem and Inspirada.

Who each one tends to fit

  • Summerlin if you want the west side, Red Rock Canyon nearby, one master association, and your budget fits the higher median.
  • Henderson if you want more price range, the choice between HOA and no HOA, or a shorter drive to the airport or Lake Mead.
  • Both have 55+ communities: Sun City Summerlin and Siena in Summerlin, Sun City Anthem, Solera and Sun City MacDonald Ranch in Henderson. Compare them on 55+ communities.
  • Schools: Clark County School District zones by address. Check any address with the CCSD zoning search, and I'll help you look it up.

Questions people ask

Is Summerlin or Henderson more expensive?

By the MLS, Summerlin. From May 14 through November 10, 2026, the median single-family sale price was $784,670 in Summerlin and $535,000 in Henderson-area ZIP codes. Henderson also had far more homes for sale under $450,000. MLS figures: Las Vegas REALTORS® MLS, not guaranteed.

Is Summerlin part of Las Vegas or Henderson?

Neither is part of the other. Most of Summerlin is in the City of Las Vegas, and Summerlin South is in unincorporated Clark County. Henderson is a separate city on the southeast side of the valley.

Which is closer to the airport?

Henderson. Most of Green Valley is about 15 minutes from Harry Reid International Airport. Summerlin is about 25 to 30 minutes away.

Which has lower property tax?

The 2026-27 rates are close: Summerlin Town 2.9328 and City of Henderson 2.9613 per $100 of assessed value, while City of Las Vegas parts of Summerlin are 3.2782. Your bill depends on the home's assessed value and the 3% cap. Check with your tax professional.
